## Abstract

$$ \psi=\psi_a+\psi_b, $$

The molecule does not create that distribution.

> enclosing organization → subordinate recurrence → enclosing occupancy readout.

The atmospheric example is not mysterious because the subordinate realizer and the enclosing distribution
can both be made readable.

Quantum interference has the same recursive architecture on the opposite side of a readability horizon.

At the quantum boundary, subordinate geometric state identity has already undergone recursive
replacement. The subordinate realization can continue to recur, but it cannot be jointly read as the same
persistent spatial object together with the enclosing quantum distribution.
