The central problem left by Addendum 66 was not whether the FLRW singularity is the primitive beginning.

R2D denies that ontology.

The remaining problem was to identify the mathematical signature by which the continuum cosmological
projection announces its own loss of recursive readability.

That signature already exists in the R2D rate construction.

$$\mathcal H_{\zeta\mid c}=\frac{d\ln a}{dN_C^{(c)}}$$

This quantity measures projected cosmological change per subordinate recurrence.

A smooth physical trajectory requires that change to be small:

$$|\mathcal H_{\zeta\mid c}|\ll1$$

Equivalently, one macroscopic logarithmic change of cosmological scale must contain many subordinate
recurrent realizations:

$$\mathcal N_{\mathrm{cont}}^{(c)}=\left|\frac{dN_C^{(c)}}{d\ln a}\right|\gg1$$

This is the count architecture beneath the continuum.
