R²D Addendum 134 — Symmetric Recurrence Across the Two Horizons

From Propagating Frequency to Mass-Bearing Energy: The Thermodynamic Doppler Hypothesis

Status: Author-approved for canon publication — November 20, 2026
Canonical basis: R²D Parts I–IV (September 25, 2026); Addenda 2, 35–36, 69–70, 105, 112, 120, and companion Addenda 131–133.
Scope: Symmetric recurrence and the two horizons of readability. Directed-closure laws and retaining-work laws are not invoked to generate the background recurrence or the proposed invariant.

Abstract

An enclosing symmetric recurrence can remain physically meaningful without producing net directional output. At a jointly readable thermal boundary, completed beats and their recurrence can both be resolved; sound illustrates how a disturbance propagates through a medium without transport of a persistent identity for each constituent. At the quantum readability horizon, the subordinate beat identities and their continuously reconstructed source geometry become unreadable while an electromagnetic frequency and propagation relation remain observable. At the gravitational readability horizon, a mass-bearing distinction remains readable, potentially as one native enclosing occurrence, while the relevant enclosing recurrence and its propagation cannot be reconstructed. Background defines the physical calibration at each boundary; the scale-free entropic organization is not an intrinsic ruler or clock.

This addendum extends the thermodynamic Doppler reciprocity of Addendum 133 to those complementary reads. It separates three objects that must not be identified: the orientation-even sum of complete turbine recurrences, the reciprocal product of opposed reference-response coordinates, and the physical frequency product of two Doppler-shifted radiation modes. It supplies an exact finite common-owner construction that admits one-sided and paired response classes and a conditional theorem: among homogeneous quadratic forms that vanish for either isolated one-sided response, the only nonzero symmetric paired norm is proportional to the product of the two responses. An eight-record example demonstrates a possible two-horizon common classification without assigning primitive mass or energy to any count.

In the separately calibrated physical representation of two oppositely propagating massless modes, the enclosing energy–momentum invariant becomes \(M^2c_\Phi^4=4h^2f_+f_-\); at zero total momentum it reads \(E_0=Mc_\Phi^2\). The system’s mass belongs to the enclosing physical relation, not as rest mass in either constituent mode. This is an established physical consistency example and agrees with Addendum 36, not a derivation of gravitational rest energy from R²D counts. The stronger gravitational inversion hypothesis—that the mass-bearing read reflects background-calibrated, unresolved symmetric dynamical organization, rather than a primitive energy substance located inside a stationary object—is formulated and sharply limited. The missing result is the native rule selecting the quadratic paired norm and its faithful energetic mass calibration before importing Lorentz geometry, \(E=hf\), or \(E=mc^2\).

134.1. The revised target: why these two physical reads?

The problem is not to prove the constancy of \(c\) across separately scaled backgrounds. It is to explain why an enclosing symmetric recurrence is physically read as a propagation speed at the quantum horizon but as mass-weighted energy at the gravitational horizon:

\[ \text{scale-free symmetric enclosing organization} \quad\rightsquigarrow\quad \begin{cases} \text{quantum: propagating frequency and }c,\\ \text{gravitational: retained distinction and }mc^2. \end{cases} \tag{134.1} \]

A physical speed and a physical rest energy have different dimensions and describe different observable classes. Their common origin, if any, must therefore be a compatibility relation plus distinct background-owned calibrations, not a primitive equation setting \(c=mc^2\). The mass \(m\) must remain in the gravitational projection.

Part III supplies complementary loss of joint readability, not destruction of the corresponding native organization. The quantum-side read can preserve spectral recurrence without a subordinate continuous trajectory. The gravitational-side read can preserve distinguishability while the relevant recursively enclosing recurrence is unavailable. The latter does not assert that all locally measurable motion, all background frequencies, or orbital periods vanish.

134.2. Symmetric recurrence is not directed closure

For a complete record \(h\) under one enclosing owner \(J\), use Addendum 131’s orientation counts:

\[ N_{\mathrm{even}}(h)=N_+(h)+N_-(h),\qquad N_{\mathrm{odd}}(h)=N_+(h)-N_-(h). \tag{134.2} \]

Symmetric realized turbine recurrence can satisfy

\[ N_+=N_->0, \qquad N_{\mathrm{even}}>0, \qquad N_{\mathrm{odd}}=0. \tag{134.3} \]

With a separately specified positive background recurrence reference \(M(h)=N_J^\Phi(h)\), its complete-return count ratio is

\[ \chi_J(h)=\frac{N_{\mathrm{even}}(h)}{M(h)}. \tag{134.4} \]

This is a dimensionless comparison of realized whole-return counts. It is not static state multiplicity, realized scalar background \(b_J\), a rate in seconds, a spectral frequency, an opposed Doppler-response product, or the energy of an imagined constituent. A complete enclosing relation can fix \(\chi_J\) on specified realized support; orientation symmetry alone does not select its value.

The directional R²D closure-asymmetry law and Part I’s retaining-work relation are not used to determine the positive background recurrence in this addendum.

134.3. Three different readings of one recursive organization

Thermodynamic joint read. At a suitable thermal observing boundary, a beat can be distinguishable and its recurrence count can be compared with the background. Sound supplies a physical illustration: a coherent pressure disturbance propagates through a material medium, while the individual medium particles do not travel from source to receiver as permanently identified carriers of that disturbance. The medium’s local motions are real subordinate realizations, not primitive owners of the enclosing collective classification.

Quantum-side read. At sufficiently rapid subordinate recurrence relative to an observing background, the individual recurring subordinate distinctions cannot be reconstructed, yet a frequency or spectral-response class can survive. Electromagnetic radiation is then read as a propagating field relation. “The counts become light” means that their recursive physical reading becomes electromagnetic frequency and propagation—not that count tokens turn into little projectiles. A source or detector can be identifiable at its own boundary without the observed field frequency uniquely reconstructing the subordinate completed-beat history. Unlike sound, electromagnetic radiation does not need a material acoustic medium; an R²D background is not a mechanical ether.

Gravitational-side read. At the opposite readability horizon, the observer may distinguish one mass-bearing enclosing occurrence while its organizing recurrence is unavailable. “One count” refers to one native readable distinction at the gravitational observing boundary, not to a claim of one constituent, one energy quantum, or only one occurrence in all possible records. The background’s nondirectional reference is not identical to the enclosing recurrence lost at this horizon.

These are proposed mutually compatible interpretations of Part III. They do not equate a measured spectral frequency with a completed turbine return rate or a mass class with the numerical number of hidden beats.

134.4. Doppler reciprocity belongs to the reference read

Addendum 133’s exact ideal axial blackbody Doppler result is

\[ T_+T_-=T_0^2, \qquad f_+f_-=f_0^2 \quad\text{for } f_i=k_BT_i/h. \tag{134.5} \]

Equivalently, in terms of the physical directional frequency read of a specified ideal pair,

\[ f_+=f_0e^{\xi},\qquad f_-=f_0e^{-\xi}. \tag{134.6} \]

Hence \(f_0=\sqrt{f_+f_-}\) remains fixed under reciprocal axial Doppler rereading. This law uses the already established relativistic physical Doppler transformation; it does not derive that transformation or \(c\) from R²D counts. Off-axis pairs do not generically obey the same exact axial product.

Three statements remain separate:

\[ \underbrace{N_++N_-}_{\text{whole-cycle even count}}, \qquad \underbrace{Z_+Z_-}_{\text{positive reference-coordinate product}}, \qquad \underbrace{f_+f_-}_{\text{physical Doppler frequency product}}. \tag{134.7} \]

Addendum 35’s positive reference coordinates and Addendum 133’s observed frequency coordinates may have compatible reciprocal representations. But an identification between them is a physical-map question, not an equality of native count types.

134.5. The smallest finite common owner with a paired response

We now give a possible finite model, not a uniquely derived R²D law. Fix a complete enclosing comparison owner \(J\) with a positive symmetric background recurrence reference \(M=12\). Every admitted comparison record carries a completed turbine count \(N_+=N_-=1\), so \(N_{\mathrm{even}}=2\), \(N_{\mathrm{odd}}=0\), and \(\chi=1/6\). These numbers are held fixed to prevent a change in directional turbine count from doing the mathematical work.

Separately, let the enclosing owner classify two opposed response coordinates

\[ u_+,\nu_-\in\mathbb N_0. \tag{134.8}\]

The \(\nu_\pm\) here are not the \(N_\pm\) completed turbine counts: they are labels of an additional directional response classification. A physical frequency read has not yet been assigned. Each admitted response pair is attached to one complete enclosing record and its valid subordinate realizations. The owner also admits orientation exchange \(\sigma(\nu_+,\nu_-)=(\nu_-,\nu_+)\).

An eight-record candidate relation is:

Complete response record \(\nu_+\) \(\nu_-\) Paired product \(P=\nu_+\nu_-\) Candidate enclosing mass class \(\mu\)
\(h_1\) 2 2 4 1
\(h_2\) 1 4 4 1
\(h_3\) 4 1 4 1
\(h_4\) 4 4 16 2
\(h_5\) 2 8 16 2
\(h_6\) 8 2 16 2
\(h_7\) 1 0 0 0
\(h_8\) 0 1 0 0

The optional classification \(\mu=\sqrt P/2\) on this chosen finite domain gives two nonzero mass-like classes and one null class. It is explicitly a trial native-class assignment, not a theorem that R²D mass is \(\sqrt{\nu_+\nu_-}/2\). A different enclosing owner could admit the same symmetric recurrence and response records while assigning a different mass classification.

Let the proposed quantum read retain the ordered response label \(q_Q(h)=(\nu_+,\nu_-)\) but not the subordinate beat identities; a physical frequency mapping remains to be supplied. Let the gravitational read retain only \(q_G(h)=\mu\). Then each nonzero gravitational class merges three different response pairs. A gravitational observer of \(\mu=1\), for example, cannot reconstruct whether its complete comparison was \((2,2)\), \((1,4)\), or \((4,1)\).

The nonconstant native quantity \(P\) factors through both selected horizon reads because the owner has assigned \(\mu\) as shown:

\[ P=P_Q\circ q_Q=P_G\circ q_G, \qquad P_G(\mu)=4\mu^2. \tag{134.9} \]

This is a valid existence construction in the sense of Addendum 132’s fiber-factorization test. Its common invariant was selected by the proposed complete pairing and mass read, not forced by symmetry or by the two horizons alone. The gravitational observer can, if the chosen rule is already known, infer \(P\) from \(\mu\) without recovering the hidden response decomposition. Mathematical inference and direct frequency observation must not be conflated.

134.6. A conditional uniqueness theorem for the quadratic paired norm

The finite candidate suggests a more exact question than whether “two dimensions” must exist. Which scalar of two reciprocal response coordinates vanishes when one side is absent, remains nonzero for a two-sided pair, and is symmetric under exchange?

Theorem 1 (unique homogeneous quadratic null-axis form). Let \(Q(x,y)\) be a real homogeneous quadratic polynomial satisfying

\[Q(x,0)=0,\qquad Q(0,y)=0\quad\text{for all }x,y.\tag{134.10}\]

Then for some constant \(\kappa\),

\[\boxed{Q(x,y)=\kappa xy.}\tag{134.11}\]

It is automatically invariant under exchange \(x\leftrightarrow y\); if it is positive for all positive paired coordinates, then \(\kappa>0\).

Proof. Write \(Q(x,y)=a x^2+bxy+d y^2\). The two one-sided null conditions give \(a=d=0\); set \(\kappa=b\). \(\square\)

This explains how a quadratic paired norm, once those premises are imposed, can arise from one-sided null responses without independently assuming two spatial axes, microscopic particles, or a mechanical trajectory. In the eight-record model, \(Q=\kappa P\) distinguishes the paired positive classes from the one-sided null classes.

Limit of the theorem. R²D symmetric recurrence does not by itself require that the physical response invariant be a quadratic polynomial, vanish on each one-sided axis, or use these particular paired response coordinates. For example, \(\min(x,y)\) is a nonnegative, symmetric, one-sided-null but nonquadratic alternative. Even within the quadratic class, \(\kappa\) is an independent normalization. The theorem derives the shape of a norm from specified requirements; it does not derive those requirements from Part I.

134.7. Physical realization: two opposed electromagnetic wave modes

We now make a separate physical mapping. Let a specified enclosing radiative system admit two oppositely propagating massless field modes with independently observed frequencies \(f_+\) and \(f_-\). Standard quantum and electromagnetic energy–momentum calibration gives

\[ \mathcal E_+=hf_+,\quad \mathcal E_-=hf_-,\qquad p_+=\frac{\mathcal E_+}{c_\Phi},\quad p_-=-\frac{\mathcal E_-}{c_\Phi}. \tag{134.12} \]

Here \(c_\Phi\) denotes the independently established propagation-speed calibration of the selected physical background. These are energies and momenta of physical modes, not primitive energies of complete count records, and not a claim that persistent microstate identities travel from emitter to detector.

The physical enclosing system has

\[ E=\mathcal E_++\mathcal E_-,\qquad p=\frac{\mathcal E_+-\mathcal E_-}{c_\Phi}. \tag{134.13} \]

Theorem 2 (two-mode invariant mass, conditional on the established physical maps). With the preceding energy–momentum calibration,

\[ \boxed{E^2-c_\Phi^2p^2=4\mathcal E_+\mathcal E_-=4h^2f_+f_-.} \tag{134.14} \]

Proof. Subtract the squares of the sum and the difference in (134.13): \((x+y)^2-(x-y)^2=4xy\). \(\square\)

If the specified enclosing invariant mass is \(M\) in the usual physical sense, then

\[ \boxed{M^2c_\Phi^4=4h^2f_+f_-.} \tag{134.15} \]

This is a direct conditional physical realization of the quadratic paired product. Each individual one-sided massless mode has the null invariant \(E^2-c_\Phi^2p^2=0\); the compatible pair can have a nonzero total invariant mass. The mass is assigned to the whole enclosing radiative system, not to either massless component. Addendum 36, Sections 32–35, already develops this result in its boundary-owned mechanical representation. This addendum does not claim it as a newly discovered relativistic identity.

The condition \(p_{\mathrm{tot}}=0\) requires equal opposing energies (hence \(f_+=f_-\) for (134.12)), not merely that the enclosing turbine have equal forward and reverse cycle counts. Write \(f_+=f_-=f_0\) in that frame. Then

\[ E_0=2hf_0,\qquad M=\frac{2hf_0}{c_\Phi^2},\qquad \boxed{E_0=Mc_\Phi^2.} \tag{134.16} \]

The gravitational-style “one retained mass-bearing read” is now the invariant mass of the enclosing system; it is not a single massless wave mode reinterpreted as a massive particle.

134.8. Doppler rereading changes direction but not the enclosing mass

Under the physical reciprocal axial Doppler relation

\[f_+=f_0e^\xi,\qquad f_-=f_0e^{-\xi},\tag{134.17}\]

we obtain

\[ E=2hf_0\cosh\xi,\qquad pc_\Phi=2hf_0\sinh\xi, \tag{134.18} \]

so

\[ E^2-c_\Phi^2p^2=4h^2f_0^2=M^2c_\Phi^4. \tag{134.19} \]

One observer resolves unequal directional frequencies and nonzero total momentum. In the enclosing zero-momentum frame, the same system has equal opposed frequencies and nonzero rest energy. The directional decomposition changes; the invariant mass-bearing relationship does not. Doppler frequency \(f_\pm\) is an observer’s read of wave modes. It is not \(N_\pm\) in Addendum 131.

This supplies the precise relationship between Addendum 133’s reciprocal thermodynamic Doppler observation and Addendum 36’s energy–momentum product. Both involve reciprocal physical coordinates, but they become a common physical representation only after separately specified read maps are established.

134.9. The gravitational inversion hypothesis

The physical result suggests the following R²D interpretation:

At the gravitational readability horizon, one mass-bearing distinction may remain observable after the enclosing symmetric dynamical recurrence that contributes to its energetic significance is no longer jointly readable. The observation is then represented as the rest energy \(mc^2\) of a stationary object, although the energetic norm belongs to the enclosing physical organization and its background calibration, not to a primitive energy substance stored inside one count.

The inversion is causal and observational. A stationary enclosing system need not be dynamically empty. A thermodynamic system can have zero mean bulk velocity while exhibiting positive internal thermal energy; likewise, the two-mode radiative example has zero total momentum and positive total energy. The background determines the physical reading; the subordinate motions realize compatible organization rather than define its primitive identity.

But three distinct claims must be kept apart:

  1. A zero-momentum composite can have nonzero invariant mass because of its internal energy. Established physical result.
  2. At a gravitational readability horizon, one retained count can be a physical mass-bearing read of an enclosing organization whose relevant recurrence is unreadable. R²D interpretive hypothesis consistent with Part III, not a universal correspondence theorem.
  3. Every mass’s entire \(mc^2\) is literally the kinetic energy of other masses moving relative to some external background. Not established, and not implied by the first two. The external observer’s frame-dependent kinetic energy cannot, without further structure, equal an invariant rest energy. Internal stresses, binding, field contributions, and a complete system boundary matter.

The careful physical proposal is therefore background-calibrated orientation-even energy of a mass-bearing enclosing system, rather than universal unobserved translational motion of independent particles. The term “rest” refers to zero total directional momentum relative to the specified comparison frame, not to cessation of recurrence or disappearance of background dynamical activity.

134.10. Different backgrounds define physical scale

Let a dimensionless complete compatibility organization \(\mathcal L\) admit more than one physically meaningful observing background, \(\Phi\) and \(\Psi\). Each may assign its own clock, ruler, and mass/energy calibrations:

\[ (\ell_\Phi,f_\Phi,m_\Phi,\varepsilon_\Phi),\qquad (\ell_\Psi,f_\Psi,m_\Psi,\varepsilon_\Psi). \tag{134.20} \]

A faithful frequency projection may read a given ratio as \(f_B^\Phi=\chi f_\Phi\) in one background and \(f_B^\Psi=\chi f_\Psi\) in another; the dimensionless count classification can survive although its measured Hertz value changes. A gravitationally readable mass classification \(\mu\) can likewise have physical reads \(m^\Phi=m_\Phi\mu\) and \(m^\Psi=m_\Psi\mu\) where the corresponding mass mapping is justified. Neither \(\chi\) nor \(\mu\) is itself a physical unit.

In the specific physical two-mode example, one background provides the propagation calibration \(c_\Phi\) and the radiative energy calibration \(h\); the resulting enclosing mass obeys (134.15). The example cannot simply be transferred unchanged to a separate gravitational background \(\Psi\) without declaring a joint enclosing comparison and the corresponding compatible physical map. The framework does not require us here to prove \(c_\Phi=c_\Psi\) or reciprocal scaling of rulers and clocks. Our question is why the respective horizon readings have the linear propagation and mass-weighted quadratic energy forms.

Nor may a numerical equality between \(f_\Phi\), a CMB thermal scale \(k_BT/h\), a structural return count, and a gravitationally hidden recurrence be presumed. Addenda 69 and 131 specifically distinguish those physical and native quantities.

134.11. What the finite construction establishes—and what it does not

Established conditional mathematics. One enclosing finite symmetric relation can admit both one-sided and paired response classes while every complete turbine record remains orientation balanced. A proposed mass classification can merge several directional response records and retain their shared paired product. If the response invariant is required to be a homogeneous quadratic form vanishing on both one-sided axes, it must be proportional to \(\nu_+\nu_-\).

Established downstream physical relation. With independently specified electromagnetic and quantum calibrations, two opposite massless modes yield an enclosing invariant
