The Cosmological Constant, de Sitter Scale, and the State-Independent Geometric Offset in the Friedmann Partition

Abstract

Addenda 61 and 62 deprojected the first two terms of the Friedmann rate constraint.

The matter-energy contribution,

\[ \frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}\varepsilon_\zeta, \]

was shown to depend on the physical energy read of cosmological peer organization divided by the physical support volume of the enclosing boundary.

For conserved pressureless source energy,

\[ F_{\mathrm{dust}}\propto A_\zeta^{-3/2}. \]

The spatial-curvature contribution,

\[ -\frac{k c^2}{a^2}, \]

was shown to be a geometric read of the evolving cosmological boundary.

For fixed curvature class,

\[ F_k\propto A_\zeta^{-1}. \]

The remaining Friedmann contribution is

\[ F_\Lambda=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}. \]

For constant Λ, this term has no dependence on the evolving cosmological boundary area:

\[ F_\Lambda\propto A_\zeta^0. \]

It therefore has different physical ownership from both matter-energy and spatial curvature.

For positive Λ, define the geometric length scale

\[ L_\Lambda\equiv\sqrt{\frac{3}{\Lambda}}. \]

Then

\[ F_\Lambda=\frac{c^2}{L_\Lambda^2}. \]

Associate with this length the reference area

\[ A_\Lambda\equiv4\pi L_\Lambda^2=\frac{12\pi}{\Lambda}. \]

Therefore,

\[ F_\Lambda=\frac{4\pi c^2}{A_\Lambda}. \]

The cosmological-constant contribution is thus an inverse-area geometric read.

But the relevant area is not the evolving cosmological macrostate area

\[ A_\zeta\]

It is the fixed reference area

\[ A_\Lambda\]

Thus:

\[ A_\Lambda\ne A_\zeta\]

The distinction is already visible in exact de Sitter geometry.

In the pure-Λ, spatially flat limit,

\[\epsilon=0,\quad k=0\]

the Friedmann constraint becomes

\[ H^2=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}=\frac{c^2}{L_\Lambda^2}\]

Define

Λ c

\[ H_\Lambda\equiv c\sqrt{\frac{\Lambda}{3}}=\frac{c}{L_\Lambda}\]

Then

\[ H^2=H_\Lambda^2\]

The first-order geometric evolution still possesses two branches:

\[ H=\pm H_\Lambda. \]

Correspondingly,

\[ a(t)=a_0e^{\pm H_\Lambda t}. \]

For a fixed comoving boundary,

\[ A_\zeta(t)\propto a^2(t), \]

so

\[ A_\zeta(t)\propto e^{\pm2H_\Lambda t}\]

The evolving cosmological area therefore changes while

\[ A_\Lambda=\frac{12\pi}{\Lambda} \]

remains constant.

Thus:

\[\Delta A_\zeta\ne0\]

while

\[\Delta A_\Lambda=0\]

The cosmological state evolves relative to a fixed geometric reference scale.

This also clarifies the causal status of Λ.

Positive Λ fixes a rate magnitude,

\[ H_\Lambda^2\]

but does not determine the sign of H .

Therefore,

\[\Lambda\ne A_\zeta\]

and

\[\Lambda\ne\Xi_\star\]

A state-independent positive cosmological constant does not supply the primitive cosmological arrow.

It contributes to the allowable magnitude of the gravitational rate after the directional branch has already been selected.

The same distinction remains in the acceleration equation.

For pure de Sitter geometry,

\[\frac{\ddot a}{a}=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}=H_\Lambda^2\]

Both

\[ a(t)=a_0e^{H_\Lambda t}\]

and

\[ a(t)=a_0e^{-H_\Lambda t}\]

satisfy

\[\frac{\ddot a}{a}=H_\Lambda^2\]

Thus positive geometric acceleration does not by itself specify expansion rather than contraction.

The directional information remains upstream.

Only after the geometric contribution has been defined may the same term be rewritten as an effective source density.

Define

\[\epsilon_\Lambda=\frac{\Lambda c^4}{8\pi G}\]

Then

8πG

\[ F_\Lambda=\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}\epsilon_\Lambda\]

If this effective component is represented as a separately conserved homogeneous perfect-fluid source, then

\[\dot\epsilon_\Lambda=0\]

The physical continuity equation,

\[\dot\epsilon_\Lambda+3H(\epsilon_\Lambda+p_\Lambda)=0\]

therefore gives, for nonzero H ,

\[ p_\Lambda=-\epsilon_\Lambda\]

Hence:

\[ w_\Lambda\equiv\frac{p_\Lambda}{\epsilon_\Lambda}=-1\]

The w = −1 vacuum-energy representation is therefore an equivalent source-coordinate representation of the fixed Λ contribution.

It does not establish that a primitive vacuum substance precedes the geometric relation.

Earlier gravitational addenda reached the same logical distinction from de Sitter horizon physics. Exact de Sitter permits Λ to be rewritten through a horizon recurrence/ruler calibration, but that result was explicitly not taken to mean that Λ is primitive recurrence, dark energy, or R2D background.

Likewise, the pure de Sitter horizon area was previously identified as a reference area rather than an invariant enclosing capacity when additional gravitational structure is present.

The same distinction now enters the cosmological Friedmann reconstruction.

The area

\[ A_\Lambda\]

sets a fixed physical reference scale of the gravitational projection.

It does not define the changing cosmological boundary.

Nor does its associated horizon recurrence define the terminal R2D clock. The earlier de Sitter analysis explicitly distinguished horizon/background recurrence from both the master directed recurrence and the missing recurrence of the open cosmological turbine.
