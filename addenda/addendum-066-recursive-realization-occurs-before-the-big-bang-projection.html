Addendum 66 — Recursive Realization Occurs Before the Big-Bang Projection

Vanishing Boundary Area, Divergent Physical Reads, and Why the FLRW Singularity Is the Opposite of R2D Origin Architecture

Abstract

Addendum 65 expressed cosmological history as changing dominance among physical Friedmann sectors along one evolving cosmological boundary-area trajectory.

For a physical sector j ,

\[ F_j(A_\zeta)=B_j A_\zeta^{-s_j}, \]

with the standard area-scaling exponents

\[ s_r=2,\qquad s_m=\frac32,\qquad s_k=1,\qquad s_\Lambda=0\]

Backward continuation of these fixed physical scaling laws produces the conventional FLRW singularity.

For any fixed nonzero comoving comparison domain,

\[ A_\zeta\propto a^2. \]

Thus:

\[ a\to0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A_\zeta\to0\]

The physical support volume then satisfies

\[ V_\zeta\propto A_\zeta^{3/2}\to0. \]

Matter density diverges:

\[ \varepsilon_m\propto A_\zeta^{-3/2}\to\infty. \]

Radiation density diverges more rapidly:

\[ \varepsilon_r\propto A_\zeta^{-2}\to\infty. \]

And, under the standard hot-source continuation,

∣H∣ → ∞.

These divergences are real properties of the extrapolated FLRW projection.

R2D does not deny the mathematics.

It denies the ontology conventionally assigned to its singular endpoint.

The singular Big Bang treats the backward limit of a later physical geometry as the primitive origin of reality:

\[ A\to0,\qquad V\to0,\qquad\epsilon\to\infty,\qquad|H|\to\infty\]

Everything is thereby assigned to an arbitrarily small physical support and the divergent endpoint is interpreted as the beginning.

R2D has the opposite causal architecture.

The largest presently realized gear is not an initially structureless point that subsequently acquires distinctions.

It is realized recursively through an infinitude of fundamental distinctions.

Those distinctions are not an infinite physical density packed into vanishing space.

Space, volume, density, temperature, curvature, and the scale factor are later physical reads of recursively organized distinction.

Thus:

[Archive box damaged: an infinitude of fundamental distinctions is distinct from divergent projected density. Author review required.]

The R2D hierarchy proceeds from primitive distinction through recursive realization:

fundamental distinctions \(\to\) subordinate closures \(\to\) recursive replacement \(\to\cdots\to B_\star\).

The largest gear \(B_\star\) is realized by this accumulated recursive organization.

Its existence is therefore not explained by extrapolating a geometric radius, scale factor, or volume backward toward zero.

Those quantities do not yet exist at the primitive level.

The singular Big-Bang ontology consequently reverses the R2D explanatory order.

It begins with the downstream projection,

a(t),

runs that projection backward to

\[ a = 0\]

and identifies the endpoint with the origin of the distinctions from which the projection itself was constructed.

R2D rejects that inversion.

The hot thermal cosmological state remains physically meaningful.

A radiation-dominated universe, thermal background, decreasing temperature under expansion, and the later cosmological history reconstructed from those physical reads can all belong to a realized cosmological boundary.

But the hot thermal boundary occurs inside an already realized recursive hierarchy.

It is not the event that creates that hierarchy.

Thus the R2D ordering is not

singular hot point → distinctions → structure → universe.

It is

fundamental distinctions→recursive realization→boundary-defined physical geometry→hot thermal cosmological regime→later cosmological history.

The Big-Bang singularity is therefore not an unresolved candidate R2D origin.

It is the singular endpoint generated when a later continuous physical projection is extrapolated backward beyond the recursive architecture that gives that projection meaning.

The central statement is:

R2D denies the singular Big Bang as the primitive origin of reality. The zero-area, infinite-density state is generated by extrapolating a later continuum cosmological projection backward until its physical support vanishes. R2D has the opposite architecture: the largest realized boundary exists through the recursive realization of an infinitude of fundamental distinctions. The hot thermal universe is a later boundary-defined physical projection within that already-realized hierarchy, not its singular beginning.

And hence:

recursive realization occurs before the Big-Bang projection.

Here “before” is ontological and causal.

It is not a statement about extending the same FLRW time coordinate to an earlier epoch.

1. Begin With the R2D Origin Architecture

R2D begins with distinguishability.

A boundary defines a count domain.

Distinctions change.

Change recurs.

Recurrence is counted.

The primitive sequence is

\[\text{boundary}\to\text{distinction}\to\text{change}\to\text{recurrence}\to\text{count}\]

No primitive spatial volume appears in this sequence.

2. Fundamental Distinctions Occur Before Cosmological Geometry

The lowest realized distinctions are not objects placed inside a previously existing spacetime.

Their recursive organization is prior to the physical spacetime read.

Thus:

\[\text{fundamental distinction}\to\text{recursive organization}\to\text{physical geometry}\]

Not:

\[\text{pre-existing geometry}\to\text{fundamental distinctions}\]

3. The Largest Gear Is Recursively Realized

Let

\[ B_\star\]

denote the largest presently realized and unclosed boundary.

Its realization depends on the complete subordinate recursive hierarchy.

It is therefore realized through an infinitude of fundamental distinctions.

4. This Infinitude Is Not a Density

The statement

\(B_\star\) is realized by an infinitude of fundamental distinctions.

does not mean

\[\epsilon\to\infty\]

Physical energy density requires both an energy read and a physical support volume.

Neither is primitive.

5. Nor Is the Infinitude Packed Into a Point

A geometric point is already a spatial concept.

R2D does not begin with infinitely many primitive distinctions compressed inside zero physical volume.

It begins before physical volume has been defined.

Thus:

\[\text{recursive infinitude}\ne\text{spatial compression}\]

6. This Is the Opposite of the Singular Big-Bang Picture

The singular FLRW extrapolation gives

\[ V \to 0\]

while physical source densities diverge.

The primitive R2D hierarchy instead begins with distinctions whose recursive realization later acquires physical support coordinates.

The two causal architectures point in opposite explanatory directions.

7. The Cosmological Boundary Area Is Already Downstream

Addendum 56 established

\[ B_\zeta \to A_\zeta\]

The physical boundary area is therefore not primitive.

8. The Scale Factor Comes Later Still

For a fixed comoving shell,

\[ A_\zeta=4\pi a^2 S_k^2(\chi)\]

Therefore:

\[ B_\zeta \to A_\zeta \to a\]

9. Running a Backward Cannot Recover the Primitive Boundary by Definition

Because a is a downstream physical coordinate,

\[ a\to 0\]

does not invert the complete chain

\[ B_\zeta \to A_\zeta \to a\]

The map is not automatically invertible to primitive ontology.

10. Nor Does Zero Area Recover Fundamental Distinction

The limit

\[ A_\zeta \to 0\]

is a limit of the geometric projection.

It is not the definition of the primitive count domain.

Thus:

Aζ = 0 ≠ fundamental R2D state.

11. Backward Cosmological Time Is Not Reverse Recursive Depth

A second inversion must also be rejected.

Moving backward along a physical cosmological clock,

\[ t\downarrow\]

is not the same operation as descending the recursive count hierarchy.

Thus:

earlier physical time≠more primitive recursive boundary.

12. In Particular

\[ t\to t_{\mathrm{sing}}\not\Rightarrow B_\zeta\to B_1\]

The time coordinate and recursive boundary index describe different relations.

13. The Big-Bang Interpretation Conflates Them

The conventional singular-origin interpretation effectively takes

\[ a(t) \to 0\]

at an earlier physical clock coordinate and reads this as the primitive origin of all physical distinction.

R2D denies that identification.

14. The Hot Thermal Universe Remains a Physical Regime

R2D does not require rejection of a hot radiation-dominated cosmological state.

It rejects the identification of that state, or its formal infinite-temperature extrapolation, with the primitive origin of recursive reality.

15. The Thermal Boundary Is Already Realized

Temperature requires a physical thermal read.

Radiation requires a physical source read.

Physical volume requires geometric support.

Therefore the hot thermal cosmological state occurs after recursive boundary realization.

16. The Correct Ordering Is

recursive realization

↓

physical cosmological boundary

↓

thermal source/support relation

↓

\[ T,\ \epsilon_r,\ a,\ H\]

17. The Hot Thermal State Cannot Create the Boundary That Makes It Readable

This is the causal inversion at the center of the Big-Bang problem.

A thermal state is defined only after there exists a physical count domain within which temperature and radiation are readable.

Therefore the thermal state cannot primitively create that same count domain.

18. Now Examine the FLRW Backward Projection

For a fixed comoving comparison shell,

\[ A_\zeta=4\pi a^2 S_k^2(\chi)\]

Thus:

\[ a\to0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A_\zeta\to0\]

Addendum 61 gave

\[ V_\zeta=\eta_{k,\chi}A_\zeta^{3/2}\]

\[ A_\zeta\to0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad V_\zeta\to0\]

For conserved pressureless physical source energy,

\[\epsilon_m=\frac{E_m}{V_\zeta}\]

Thus:

\[\epsilon_m\propto A_\zeta^{-3/2}\to\infty\]

21. Radiation Density Diverges Faster

For radiation,

\[ w_r=\frac13\]

so:

\[\epsilon_r\propto A_\zeta^{-2}\to\infty\]

4πkSk2 (χ)

\[|K_\zeta|\propto A_\zeta^{-1}\to\infty\]

for

\[ k \ne 0\]

Where radiation dominates,

\[ H^2\sim B_rA_\zeta^{-2}\]

Therefore:

\[|H|\sim\sqrt{B_r}\,A_\zeta^{-1}\to\infty\]

24. The Mathematical Singularity Is Real Within the Projection

The FLRW projection genuinely produces

\[ V \to 0\]

\[\epsilon \to \infty\]

\[|H|\to\infty\]

and divergent spacetime curvature measures.

R2D does not need these divergences to disappear mathematically.

25. Curved Spacetime Is Itself a Projection

The crucial point is instead that spacetime curvature is already a mature gravitational read of recursively organized boundary relations.

Thus:

divergent curved spacetime = divergent gravitational projection.

It is not a divergence of primitive geometry, because R2D contains no primitive curved spacetime.

26. Therefore the Spacetime Singularity Is Not Primitive Reality Becoming Singular

The relation is

singular projected curvature≠singular primitive distinguishability.

More strongly, R2D denies the latter as the origin architecture.

The backward extrapolation retains:

\[\pi_\zeta\]

\[\Pi_G\]

\[ V(A)\]

\[ w_j\]

\[ k\]

\[ A_\zeta \to 0\]

The singularity therefore answers:

What happens if the same continuum cosmological read is mathematically continued toward zero support?

The answer is divergence.

29. It Does Not Answer the R2D Origin Question

The R2D question is:

What recursive count architecture must exist before that continuum physical read can exist at all?

That question has already been answered in the opposite direction:

fundamental distinctions → recursive realization → physical projection.

30. The Big Bang Reverses That Ordering

The singular-origin interpretation instead reads:

zero physical projection → all subsequent distinction.

R2D rejects this reversal.

31. The Singularity Is Therefore a Projection Endpoint, Not an Origin Candidate

The correct R2D statement is not merely:

the Big Bang might not be primitive.

It is:

the singular Big Bang is not the primitive R2D origin.

Primitive recursive realization does not require

\[ V = 0\]

\[ T = \infty\]

ε = ∞,

or

\[|H|\to\infty\]

None of those quantities exists at the primitive count level.

33. The Largest Gear Is Not Born Small

This is a particularly important inversion.

The largest gear is not a tiny early version of the present physical universe.

“Large” and “small” in the spatial sense are already projected coordinates.

The largest gear is largest by recursive enclosure and realized distinction, not by a primitive metric radius.

34. Therefore Cosmological Size and Recursive Depth Must Not Be Confused

The relation

\[ a_{\mathrm{early}}<a_{\mathrm{late}}\]

does not imply

\[\text{early universe}=\text{less recursively realized reality}\]

The two statements belong to different coordinate systems.

Likewise,

\[ B_n \to B_{n+1} \ne a \to a + \Delta a\]

Expansion occurs within a projected boundary.

Recursive realization changes count domain.

36. The Big-Bang Singularity Results From Confusing These Two Orderings

Backward geometric contraction is treated as though it were ontological deconstruction.

R2D denies that identification.

37. The Hot Big-Bang Ruler Has a Finite Domain of Meaning

A thermal cosmological ruler requires a realized thermal boundary.

It cannot be extrapolated through a boundary at which the thermal classification itself does not exist.

\[ a = 0\]

\[ T \to \infty\]

cannot describe uninterrupted thermal history back to the primitive origin.

The thermal projection has already changed domain.

40. The Area-First Argument Is More General

The present result does not depend on temperature.

\[ A_\zeta\]

is downstream of boundary realization.

Therefore zero projected area cannot define primitive origin.

Let

\[\mathcal I_{\mathrm{FLRW}}\]

denote the interval of boundary area over which the fixed FLRW physical projection remains valid.

This is the domain over which the same state ownership and physical maps remain readable.

For every physical observable Q,

\[ Q\circ\Pi_G=\bar Q_\zeta\circ\pi_\zeta\]

must remain valid.

If this factorization fails, Q no longer represents the same cosmological macrostate classification.

If

\[\pi_\zeta\]

is replaced by a different count classification, then the old physical coordinates cannot simply be continued unchanged.

The correct relation becomes:

old boundary → loss of old readability → replacement boundary → new physical projection.

44. This Is Not Optional If the Count Domain Changes

Recursive replacement removes the old state identity.

A new boundary defines a new count domain.

Therefore the previous continuum variables do not possess automatic cross-boundary identity.

45. The Zero-Area State Is Not Needed

Once boundary replacement is recognized, nothing requires the previous projection to reach

\[ A_\zeta = 0\]

The singular point belongs to the mathematical continuation of the superseded coordinates.

46. R2D Does Not Yet Need a Numerical Breakdown Area

The present result does not require specifying

\[ A_{\mathrm{br}}\]

The ontology already excludes the zero-area singular state as primitive origin.

The next problem is to identify the mathematical signature of the actual projection replacement.

47. No Planck Cutoff Is Inserted by Hand

Nothing here requires

\[ A_{\mathrm{br}}=\ell_P^2\]

A numerical crossover must emerge from the physical mapping, not from imposing a conventional microscopic ruler.

48. R2D Also Does Not Require a Bounce

Rejecting the singular Big Bang does not imply

\[ H < 0 \to H = 0 \to H > 0\]

A bounce is one possible physical projection.

It is not the primitive R2D statement.

49. Nor Does It Require a Pre-Big-Bang FLRW Branch

There need not be an earlier extension of the same scale factor or the same cosmological time coordinate.

If the boundary changes, the earlier physical read may belong to a different projection altogether.

50. “Before the Big Bang” Is Therefore Potentially Misleading

R2D does not necessarily posit another FLRW universe at

\[ t<t_{\mathrm{BB}}\]

It says that recursive realization is ontologically prior to the physical coordinates in which a Big-Bang time is defined.

51. The Distinction Is Causal, Not Merely Temporal

Thus:

\[\text{recursive prior}\ne\text{earlier value of }t\]

This is essential.

One projected scale is driven toward zero.

Then:

\[ V \to 0\]

source densities diverge,

\[ H^2\]

diverges,

and spacetime-curvature reads diverge.

These are coupled consequences of one extrapolation.

53. They Are Not Independent Evidence for a Singular Primitive Event

The simultaneous divergence of many projected quantities does not provide multiple independent demonstrations of primitive singularity.

The variables are mathematically linked inside the same physical projection.

For every positive sector with

\[ s_j > 0\]

\[ F_j=B_jA^{-s_j}\]

Therefore:

\[\lim_{A\to0^+}F_j=\infty\]

Once the scaling law is assumed to persist, divergence is guaranteed.

55. The Divergence Therefore Tests the Assumption at Its Limit

It does not independently validate that assumption.

The singularity is what the fixed projection predicts when extended without replacement.

The singular endpoint therefore occurs at finite projected clock time.

58. Finite Projected Time Does Not Make the Endpoint Primitive

The time coordinate belongs to the same physical projection whose domain is under examination.

59. A Clock Cannot Establish the Origin of the Boundary That Defines the Clock

This repeats the same causal inversion.

This is the central diagnosis.

The mathematical sequence is valid:

\[ A\downarrow\to V\downarrow\to\epsilon\uparrow\to|H|\uparrow\]

The ontological conclusion is not.

61. The R2D Inversion Is

Conventional singular-origin reading:

zero geometric support → all later structure.

R2D:

fundamental distinctions → recursive realization → physical support geometry.

The explanatory arrows are reversed.

62. Principle — Recursive Infinitude Is Not Infinite Density

The largest realized gear is realized by an infinitude of fundamental distinctions.

Those distinctions are not a physical substance divided by a spatial volume.
