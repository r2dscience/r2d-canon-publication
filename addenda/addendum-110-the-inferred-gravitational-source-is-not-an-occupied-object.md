### Abstract

A measured gravitational discrepancy is real. A source term inferred by holding a gravitational calibration fixed is also a well-defined result of that calculation. Neither statement makes the inferred term an independently occupied kind of matter. This addendum identifies the exact inferential step at which an apparent dark-matter source is assigned physical identity and the subsequent step at which that assigned source is made the primitive cause of the gravitational relation it was introduced to represent.

R²D Part IV already derives an exact source–calibration equivalence within a stated simple gravitational relation. If a system at boundary $B_n$ is read with gravitational calibration $G_n$ but interpreted using $G_{n-1}$, then the inferred dynamical mass is $M_{\mathrm{dyn}}=(G_n/G_{n-1})M_b$. Under the Canon's invariant dimensionless gravitational calibration hypothesis, the apparent dark component satisfies $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}/M_b=(\rho_{n\mid n-1}^{\Phi})^{-2}-1$. The same recurrence ratio has a complementary geometric projection. These are mathematical relationships with stated physical premises, not a census of occupied invisible objects.

The first projection error is the promotion of $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}$, a source coordinate of the selected gravitational representation, into an independently occupied matter state without a boundary-defined identity relation. The second is to assign that projected state primitive causal priority over the enclosing organization and calibration that gave the source coordinate its meaning. The observed gravitational relations and the useful calculations remain in place. The full application of R²D to galaxies, lensing, clusters, and cosmology is a separate physical mapping; it does not have to be completed before the first inferential error can be identified.

## 110.1. What Is Actually Read

The gravitational observation is a relation among physical reads. In a simple approximately spherical, weak-field presentation, let $g_{\mathrm{obs}}(r)$ be the acceleration read, $r$ the selected radial coordinate, and $M_b(r)$ the baryonic source read. These quantities are not interchangeable. The observed acceleration is not itself a measurement of the composition of every compatible realization of the gravitating system.

Rotation measurements established persistent gravitational discrepancies across galaxies. Lensing of the merging cluster 1E0657-558 established a spatial separation between a gravitational lensing reconstruction and the dominant X-ray-emitting plasma. CMB temperature, polarization, and lensing measurements support a precisely fitted nonbaryonic component within the base-$\Lambda$CDM model. These are distinct gravitational and cosmological reads; none is made into a direct inventory of particle identities by calling its fitted term “matter.” [6–8]

The question here is exact: **What has been established when a successful gravitational representation contains an extra source term, and at what point is that term made into an occupied object?** The term *object* in the title includes a hypothesized matter component, field, or particle population. No particle hypothesis is needed for the first source-identity error.

## 110.2. The Canon's Source–Calibration Equivalence

Part IV §10.35 begins with a specified physical assignment: at background boundary $B_n$, the simple gravitational relation is

$$
g_{\mathrm{obs}}(r)=G_n\frac{M_b(r)}{r^2}.
$$

An observer using the reference calibration $G_{n-1}$ defines an inferred dynamical mass by

$$
g_{\mathrm{obs}}(r)=G_{n-1}\frac{M_{\mathrm{dyn}}(r)}{r^2}.
$$

Equating the two descriptions of the same acceleration gives

$$
M_{\mathrm{dyn}}(r)=\frac{G_n}{G_{n-1}}M_b(r),
\qquad
M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}(r)
:=M_{\mathrm{dyn}}(r)-M_b(r)
=\left(\frac{G_n}{G_{n-1}}-1\right)M_b(r).
$$

This is the exact algebraic result under the stated simple relation. The superscript $\mathrm{app}$ matters: $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}$ names what appears as additional source **under the chosen calibration**. Its definition does not say that a second material component has been observed occupying that amount of mass.

Part IV §10.34 defines a dimensionless gravitational calibration $\gamma_{G,n}=G_nh(f_n^\Phi)^2/c^5$. If adjacent background boundaries are in the same gravitational projection class and $\gamma_{G,n}=\gamma_{G,n-1}$, then, with $\rho_{n\mid n-1}^\Phi=f_n^\Phi/f_{n-1}^\Phi$,

$$
\frac{G_n}{G_{n-1}}
=\left(\rho_{n\mid n-1}^{\Phi}\right)^{-2},
\qquad
\frac{M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}}{M_b}
=\left(\rho_{n\mid n-1}^{\Phi}\right)^{-2}-1.
$$

The assumption about $\gamma_G$ is part of the physical calibration bridge. It is not supplied merely by defining a microstate. Likewise, $n$ indexes recursive background boundaries; the equation does not assert a change in $G$ over ordinary clock time within one boundary.

The observational relation fixes a *product* in this simple description. Reading it with a fixed $G_{n-1}$ assigns the difference to source; reading it with $M_b$ fixed assigns the difference to calibration. The observation by itself does not select which factorization has physical priority.

## 110.3. The Exact Point of Physical-Identity Promotion

Three statements must be kept distinct:

| Statement | What establishes it |
|---|---|
| There is a measured gravitational relation that the selected baryonic model must explain. | Gravitational observations and their stated measurement procedures. |
| Holding a specified gravitational calibration fixed yields $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}$. | The gravitational equations and the selected calibration. |
| $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}$ is an independently occupied matter state with its own physical identity. | An additional physical identification, not the definition of $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}$. |

The inferential crossing occurs at the third statement. The notation $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}$ supplies a source coordinate; it does not supply a boundary, a compatible realization domain, or an occupation relation for a new material component. Replacing “mass required in this representation” with “matter physically occupying the missing mass” changes the type of claim.

The same issue occurs if a fitted additional stress–energy term $T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{DM}}$ is introduced into a gravitational equation. Its ability to reproduce a gravitational read is an achievement of the specified model. Occupied state identity does not follow from the tensor's successful role as a source coordinate. A theory that identifies it with material constituents supplies another physical premise and must say what that premise is.

This is the **first gravitational projection error**: a representation of a readable consequence is assigned the identity of an independently occupied object across a relation the representation itself has not made readable. The error occurs at the identification, before the further question of what the proposed object causes.

## 110.4. The Boundary Test for Occupation

R²D does not define a microstate by the name of a fitted variable. At an operative count boundary $B$, complete compatible subordinate realizations form $\mathfrak M_B$, and a classification map defines its macrostates:

$$
\pi_B:\mathfrak M_B\longrightarrow\mathcal M_B,
\qquad
W_{B,i}=\bigl|\pi_B^{-1}(i)\bigr|.
$$

To call something an occupied realization of $i$ at $B$ is to assign it to the physically relevant classified domain and its occupation. The number $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}$ is calculated from a gravitational read; it is not an element of $\pi_B^{-1}(i)$ and does not itself define such an element. No count of independently occupied dark constituents follows from subtracting $M_b$ from $M_{\mathrm{dyn}}$.

Addendum 105 establishes why a subordinate state reconstructed by changing the operative read cannot be used to invert the causal priority of the enclosing classification. Here the reconstruction goes further: a gravitationally inferred difference is given an independent material identity before its compatible state domain is specified. Part IV's primitive-object analysis identifies the resulting category error. [1, 3, 5]

The gravitational-horizon read makes the temptation intelligible. The enclosing directional recurrence organizing gravity is unreadable at that horizon, while gravitational distinctions remain readable. A successful source representation describes those surviving distinctions. **Unreadable enclosing recurrence is not a warrant to manufacture an occupied matter state from the source term used to represent its effects.** This dark-matter application uses the Canon's gravitational readability architecture; it does not assert that a galaxy's mass discrepancy is itself a black-hole horizon.

## 110.5. The Second Error: Giving the Projection Primitive Causal Agency

Once the additional source has been assigned material identity, the source can be said to produce the gravitational organization that required its introduction. The descriptive direction is then silently inverted:

$$
\text{observed gravitational relation}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{inferred source coordinate}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{independent material cause}.
$$

R²D orders the explanation differently. An enclosing compatibility relation determines which subordinate realizations and gravitational reads are available. The physical gravitational equation is a representation of that realized organization. A source coordinate extracted *using* that equation does not, as that coordinate, primitively define the enclosing relation that makes the equation applicable.

This second error is an EP causal inversion. It is separate from the first assignment of occupied identity. One may compute an apparent mass without making either error; the valid calculation is exactly what allows the inferential steps to be located. Part IV §§1.14–1.17 and Addenda 39, 55, and 105 give the general object, source, and organizing-causality ordering. [1, 3–5]

## 110.6. Source and Geometry Are Complementary Reads

Part IV §10.36 expresses the same mismatch with areal calibration. With $\mathcal A$ the specified gravitational area read,

$$
g=4\pi G_n\frac{M}{\mathcal A}
=4\pi G_{n-1}\frac{M}{\mathcal A_{\mathrm{eff}}}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{eff}}
=\mathcal A\frac{G_{n-1}}{G_n}
=\mathcal A\left(\rho_{n\mid n-1}^{\Phi}\right)^2.
$$

Consequently, for the same simple relation,

$$
\frac{M_{\mathrm{dyn}}}{M_b}
=\frac{\mathcal A}{\mathcal A_{\mathrm{eff}}}
=\left(\rho_{n\mid n-1}^{\Phi}\right)^{-2}.
$$

A discrepancy represented as extra source under one fixed calibration can be represented as changed areal calibration under another. This equivalence does not make the physical mapping arbitrary: the boundary, the gravitational read, and the recurrence ratio have to be specified. It does show why a source coordinate cannot be promoted to occupied matter solely because the original source representation fit the data.

A constant $\rho^\Phi$ gives a rescaling in this construction. A spatially varying $\rho^\Phi(r)$ enters the areal-radius relation $R_{\mathcal A}(r)=\rho^\Phi(r)r$ and requires a more developed geometric account. Neither one-dimensional algebraic identity alone specifies a full covariant theory of lensing or cosmological perturbations. [3]

## 110.7. What Would Establish the Broader Physical Mapping

The formal identity issue and the astrophysical mapping have different tests. The first is settled by the definition of the inferred term: $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}$ is a coordinate calculated under a selected gravitational representation, not an occupation measurement. The second asks whether the R²D recurrence-calibration construction accounts for the relevant observations as a whole.

Part IV lists galaxy rotation, lensing, clusters, structure formation, cosmological gravitational observations, and their relation to baryonic structure. The observations are not one scalar measurement repeated six times. The Bullet Cluster's separation of lensing and plasma, for example, requires a spatial gravitational account; the CMB fit involves perturbations and a cosmological model. A radius-dependent scalar replacement of $G$ in the equation of §110.2 is not already such an account. [3, 7, 8]

There is also a specific circularity to avoid. Algebraically inverting a measured discrepancy gives

$$
\rho_{n\mid n-1}^{\Phi}
=\sqrt{\frac{M_b}{M_{\mathrm{dyn}}}}
$$

when the positive quantities and Canon's stated range apply. If $\rho^\Phi$ is defined this way from the very mass discrepancy to be explained, the agreement in that system is algebraic, not a new independent prediction. A physical R²D mapping would assign the background relation through specified boundary conditions, then carry that assignment through the gravitational, geometric, lensing, and cosmological reads without merely choosing a new $\rho^\Phi$ for each result. This is a demand on the *physical application*, not a demand for an experiment to prove what an inferred coordinate means by definition.

The range also constrains the proposed assignment. Under the Canon's $0<\rho^\Phi<1$ and invariant-$\gamma_G$ premises, the relation predicts $M_{\mathrm{dyn}}/M_b>1$ for the comparison being made. A specified system and boundary assignment that require the opposite ratio do not fit this particular calibration bridge. Renaming the fitted mass ratio as $\rho^\Phi$ would conceal rather than resolve that failure.

The same discipline applies to an occupied-matter model: its source distribution, dynamics, and observational reads must be supplied as physical premises. A successful fit does not retroactively turn the fitted source coordinate into a direct observation of its constituents. This is a symmetric distinction between mathematical representation and physical identity.

This resolves the apparent tension with Part IV §10.74. The Canon has already established that an apparent extra source is a projection of a calibration difference **within the stated simple relation**. Section 10.74 addresses the further claim that this construction accounts for the whole observational domain called dark matter. The exact source-coordinate result and the scope of the physical application are different propositions; neither cancels the other.

## 110.8. Relation to the Other Object Projections

The common structure is a promotion from a successful representation to an occupation claim:

| Case | Surviving read | Introduced representation | Unlicensed identity step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quantum particle | Enclosing quantum read with subordinate identity unreadable | Particle state or trajectory | Representation of an unreadable subordinate distinction becomes a persistent occupied particle. |
| Everettian branch | Quantum amplitude and branch structure | Branch of a representation | Branch label becomes a separately occupied world. |
| Causal string | Horizon charge and leading entropy count | Selected string or D-brane state sector | Counted state label becomes an occupied horizon realization. |
| Apparent dark matter | Gravitational relation read with a selected calibration | Additional source term $M_{\mathrm{DM}}^{\mathrm{app}}$ or $T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{DM}}$ | Inferred source becomes independently occupied matter. |

The cases do not share one physical horizon or one equation. They share a logical step. A representation can accurately preserve a surviving read and still leave the identity and occupation of the represented side undefined. The second, causal mistake occurs if the newly assigned object is made to generate the boundary relation whose projection introduced it. [1, 2, 5, 9]

## 110.9. Result and Status

**Formal result.** A source coordinate defined by inversion of a gravitational relation under a fixed calibration has no occupied matter identity by virtue of that definition. In R²D, the classification and compatible realization domain required for an occupation claim belong to an operative boundary. The fitted coordinate does not supply them. [1, 5]

**Exact conditional equivalence.** Part IV's simple relation gives $M_{\mathrm{dyn}}/M_b=G_n/G_{n-1}=(\rho^\Phi)^{-2}$ under its stated recurrence-calibration premise. It also yields the complementary areal projection. These equalities preserve the measured relation without turning the inferred source into observed material. [3]

**First projection error.** The mathematical or fitted apparent source is asserted to be an independently occupied matter state solely because the source representation succeeds.

**Second causal inversion.** That asserted object is then given primitive priority over the enclosing state classification and physical calibration that its source coordinate presupposes.

**Physical application.** Whether a specified recurrence-calibration mapping accounts for the complete observational pattern assigned to dark matter is a quantitative, cross-observable question. The simple equivalence neither supplies that complete mapping nor weakens the formal result about the source coordinate's identity.

**The observed gravitational discrepancy is real. Its inferred source term is real mathematics. The step from that term to an occupied material cause is an additional claim, not a consequence of the gravitational fit.**
