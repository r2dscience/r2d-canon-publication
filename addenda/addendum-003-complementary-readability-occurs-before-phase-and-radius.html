Addendum 3 — Complementary Readability Occurs Before Phase and Radius

Recursive Replacement, the h/G Conversion Duality, and the Planck Crossover

Abstract

Recursively Realized Distinguishability begins neither with quantum phase nor with gravitational radius.

Both are later physical coordinates.

At a recursively realized boundary \(B_n\), two primitive closure reads are distinguished:

\[\lambda_n\]

for readable distinction, and

\[\nu_n\]

for recurrent count.

Thermodynamics is the regime in which both sides of this closure architecture can remain jointly readable.

Quantum and gravitational physics are complementary incomplete reads.

At a quantum readability horizon, subordinate spatial distinguishability has been recursively replaced while recurrence remains readable:

\[\lambda_{\mathrm{sub}}\text{ unreadable},\qquad \nu\text{ readable}.\]

The surviving recurrent relation is represented through cyclic phase:

\[\nu\to\phi.\]

Only after physical calibration does recurrence acquire the energetic read

\(E=hf\)

or, in angular coordinates,

\(E=\hbar\omega\).

At a gravitational readability horizon the complementary situation appears.

Detailed subordinate recurrence is no longer jointly readable, while the enclosing distinguishable spatial relation remains accessible:

\[\lambda\text{ readable},\qquad \nu_{\mathrm{sub}}\text{ unreadable}.\]

The surviving relation is represented geometrically through boundary area and its derived radial coordinate:

\[\mathcal A\to r_A=\sqrt{\frac{\mathcal A}{4\pi}}.\]

The corresponding mass-energy read acquires a gravitational length calibration

\[\ell_G=\frac{Gm}{c^2}=\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

Thus phase and radius occupy complementary projection roles.

They are not the same object:

\[\phi\ne r.\]

Rather,

\[\phi\longleftrightarrow r.\]

because each is a downstream coordinate placed on the side of recursive closure that remains physically readable after its complement has been replaced.

The constants \(h\) and \(G\) occupy a corresponding pair of conversion roles.

They do not cause recursive replacement.

Recursive replacement belongs to the count architecture.

Instead,

\[h,\hbar\]

assign energetic place value to readable recurrence, while

\[G\]

assigns geometric place value to a mass-energy read of recursively accumulated structure.

For an angular recurrence frequency \(\omega\), define the recurrence ruler

\[\ell_\omega=\frac{c}{\omega}.\]

Using

\(E=\hbar\omega\),

this becomes

\[\ell_\omega=\frac{\hbar c}{E}.\]

The gravitational ruler assigned to that same energetic read is

\[\ell_G=\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

Their product is

\[\ell_\omega\ell_G=\frac{G\hbar}{c^3}=\ell_P^2.\]

The algebraic identity is conventional.

Its R²D interpretation is not.

The Planck area is the invariant product of the quantum recurrence ruler and the gravitational geometry ruler assigned to the same physical energetic distinction.

As energy increases,

\(\ell_\omega\propto E^{-1}\),

while

\(\ell_G\propto E\).

The complementary rulers therefore vary reciprocally while preserving

\(\ell_\omega\ell_G=\ell_P^2\).

At their crossover,

\[\ell_\omega=\ell_G,\]

so

\(\ell_\omega=\ell_G=\ell_P\).

R²D therefore interprets the Planck scale not first as a smallest material length or a singular new substance, but as the crossover at which the two complementary physical projection rulers become equal.

The central statement is:

Quantum and gravitational physics are complementary incomplete reads of the same recursively realized closure architecture. Quantum projection preserves recurrence after subordinate geometry has become unreadable and represents that recurrence through phase. Gravitational projection preserves enclosing spatial distinguishability after subordinate recurrence has become unreadable and represents that relation through area and radius. Phase and radius are therefore complementary projection coordinates, while \(h\) and \(G\) occupy complementary calibration roles. Their associated rulers satisfy \(\ell_\omega\ell_G=\ell_P^2\), identifying the Planck scale as the crossover of the two opposite readability projections.

And hence:

Complementary readability occurs before phase and radius.

1. Begin With the Recursive Closure Coordinates

R²D does not begin with:

wave

or

radius.

It begins with boundary-defined distinguishability and recurrence.

At boundary \(B_n\), write the distinguishable interval as

\[\lambda_n,\]

and the recurrent count as

\[\nu_n.\]

2. These Coordinates Have Different Readability Regimes

The central R²D classification is:

\[ \boxed{ \begin{array}{c|cc} & \lambda & \nu \\ \hline \text{Thermodynamic} & \text{readable} & \text{readable} \\ \text{Quantum} & \text{unreadable} & \text{readable} \\ \text{Gravitational} & \text{readable} & \text{unreadable} \end{array} } \]

This table is not a taxonomy added after the physical theories have been constructed.

It predicts that different physical coordinate systems should appear when opposite parts of closure remain readable.

3. Thermodynamics Exposes the Paired Architecture Most Directly

In the thermodynamic regime, both:

distinguishable state structure

and

recurrent realization

remain jointly accessible.

Thus thermodynamics can reveal both sides of the turbine relation.

This is one reason thermodynamics occupies a privileged position in R²D.

4. Quantum Physics Loses the Spatial Side First

At the quantum readability horizon, the subordinate geometry that would identify a continuously persistent object or trajectory is no longer jointly readable.

Schematically:

\[\lambda_{\mathrm{sub}}\to\text{unreadable}.\]

5. Recurrence Can Nevertheless Remain Readable

The enclosing quantum boundary can still retain a recurrent relation:

\[\nu\to\text{readable}.\]

Thus loss of subordinate geometry does not imply loss of recurrence.

6. Recursive Replacement Is Not Hidden Coarse-Graining

The subordinate object does not remain as a fully formed smaller version of the same state waiting to be resolved.

Its state identity belongs to its own boundary.

At the enclosing boundary that identity has already been replaced.

Therefore:

subordinate state identity does not equal enclosing state identity.

7. The Surviving Recurrence Can Be Represented Cyclically

A closed recurrence admits a cyclic coordinate.

Write:

\[\phi\]

for that phase coordinate.

Thus:

\[\text{closure}\to\text{recurrence}\to\phi.\]

8. Phase Is Therefore Not Primitive

The phase coordinate represents recurrent closure.

It does not create the closure.

Thus:

\[\text{closure}\ne\phi.\]

9. Nor Is Phase a Hidden Spatial Trajectory

The cyclic coordinate

\[\phi\]

does not reconstruct the subordinate geometry that has already been recursively replaced.

It is the readable representation of the recurrent side of closure.

10. Physical Frequency Is a Later Clock Read

Once a physical clock calibration is supplied, recurrence can be represented by a cycle frequency

\[f\]

or angular frequency

\[\omega=2\pi f.\]

11. Planck Calibration Converts Cycle Recurrence Into Energy

For completed cycles,

\(E=hf\).

The constant

\[h\]

assigns energetic place value to the physical recurrence frequency.

12. Angular Phase Uses the Reduced Constant

Because

\[\omega=2\pi f,\]

one writes

\(E=\hbar\omega\),

with

\[\hbar=\frac{h}{2\pi}.\]

13. \(h\) Does Not Cause Recursive Replacement

The causal ordering is not

\[h\to\text{quantization}.\]

It is:

\[\text{recursive replacement}\to\text{readable recurrence}\to(f,\omega,\phi)\to E.\]

14. \(h\) Is a Physical Conversion Constant

Thus:

\(h\) = cycle-recurrence-to-energy calibration,

while

\(\hbar\) = angular-phase-to-energy calibration.

Their numerical universality is an empirical property of the quantum physical projection.

15. Gravity Loses the Complementary Side

At a gravitational readability horizon, detailed subordinate recurrence is no longer jointly accessible.

Schematically:

\[\nu_{\mathrm{sub}}\to\text{unreadable}.\]

16. Enclosing Spatial Distinguishability Remains

The larger boundary can nevertheless retain a spatial/geometric distinction.

Thus:

\[\lambda\to\text{readable}.\]

17. The Geometric Read Begins With Boundary Area

R²D places area before radius.

Let the relevant enclosing boundary area be

\[\mathcal A.\]

18. Radius Is a Later Representation of Area

For an isotropic spherical read, define

\[r_A=\sqrt{\frac{\mathcal A}{4\pi}}.\]

Thus:

\[\mathcal A\to r_A,\]

not primitively

\[r\to\mathcal A.\]

19. Radius Is Therefore Not Primitive Geometry

The radius is a coordinate used to represent an already-established enclosing spatial relation.

Thus:

\[\text{boundary relation}\to\mathcal A\to r.\]

20. Mass-Energy Acquires a Gravitational Length Read

Define

\[\ell_G=\frac{Gm}{c^2}.\]

Using

\[E=mc^2,\]

this may also be written

\[\ell_G=\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

21. \(\ell_G\) Is Not Automatically a Material Radius

The gravitational conversion length does not by itself assert that the source is a sphere of radius

\[\ell_G.\]

It is the length scale assigned to the mass-energy read by the gravitational physical projection.

22. \(G\) Does Not Cause Recursive Replacement

The ordering is not

\[G\to\text{gravity}.\]

Rather:

\[\text{recursive enclosing structure}\to\text{mass-energy read}\to\text{geometric calibration}.\]

23. \(G\) Is Therefore a Geometric Conversion Constant

Within this physical mapping,

\[G\]

assigns geometric place value to mass-energy.

More precisely,

\[\frac{G}{c^4}\]

converts energetic read into a length scale.

24. Quantum and Gravity Are Complementary Projections

Quantum:

\[\lambda_{\mathrm{sub}}\text{ unreadable},\qquad \nu\text{ readable}.\]

Gravity:

\[\lambda\text{ readable},\qquad \nu_{\mathrm{sub}}\text{ unreadable}.\]

25. Their Familiar Coordinates Therefore Arise on Opposite Sides

Quantum recurrence is represented through

\[\phi.\]

Gravitational distinguishability is represented through

\[\mathcal A,r.\]

26. This Creates a Projection Parallel

\(\phi\leftrightarrow r\).

This does not mean the two coordinates are mathematically identical.

27. Their Parallel Is Their Logical Position

Phase is the later coordinate of readable recurrence.

Radius is the later coordinate of readable enclosing geometry.

Thus:

\[ \boxed{ \begin{array}{c|c} \text{Quantum} & \text{Gravity} \\ \hline \text{recurrence} & \text{spatial distinction} \\ \downarrow & \downarrow \\ \phi & \mathcal A\rightarrow r \end{array} } \]

28. Neither Coordinate Should Be Promoted Back Into Primitive Ontology

Therefore:

Phase is not primitive quantum substance,

and

radius is not primitive gravitational substance.

Both are extremely useful physical coordinates.

Neither is the recursively realized object beneath the projection.

29. The Conversion Constants Also Occupy Complementary Roles

Quantum:

\[\text{recurrence}\xrightarrow{h,\hbar}\text{energy}.\]

Gravity:

\[\text{energy}\xrightarrow{G/c^4}\text{geometric length}.\]

30. Energy Is the Shared Mature Coordinate Between the Two Reads

The sequence can therefore be written

\[\omega\xrightarrow{\hbar}E\xrightarrow{G/c^4}\ell_G.\]

31. This Does Not Make Energy a More Primitive R²D Substance

Energy is a physical calibration coordinate.

The relation above does not require one primitive energetic object to be transported unchanged from quantum recurrence into gravitational geometry.

32. The Shared Coordinate Allows the Two Projections to Be Compared

For a physical energetic distinction \(E\), the quantum side assigns a recurrence scale while the gravitational side assigns a geometry scale.

This permits a direct comparison.

33. Define the Angular Recurrence Ruler

Let

\[\ell_\omega=\frac{c}{\omega}.\]

This is the spatial ruler assigned to an angular recurrence through the common propagation calibration c.

34. Express It in Terms of Energy

Using

\(E=\hbar\omega\),

one has

\[\ell_\omega=\frac{\hbar c}{E}.\]

35. The Gravitational Ruler for the Same Energy Is

\[\ell_G=\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

36. Multiply the Complementary Rulers

\[\ell_\omega\ell_G=\frac{\hbar c}{E}\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

The energetic coordinate cancels.

Thus:

\[\ell_\omega\ell_G=\frac{G\hbar}{c^3}.\]

37. Define the Planck Length

\[\ell_P=\sqrt{\frac{G\hbar}{c^3}}.\]

Therefore:

\(\ell_\omega\ell_G=\ell_P^2\).

38. The Identity Is Conventional; the Interpretation Is R²D

The algebra does not require R²D.

What R²D adds is the recognition that the two factors are the characteristic rulers of the two complementary readability projections.

39. Thus the Planck Area Has a New Interpretation

\[\ell_P^2=\text{quantum recurrence ruler}\times\text{gravitational geometry ruler}.\]

More precisely:

The Planck area is the invariant product of the two complementary physical rulers assigned to one energetic distinction.

40. The Complementary Rulers Scale Oppositely

From

\[\ell_\omega=\frac{\hbar c}{E},\]

\(\ell_\omega\propto E^{-1}\).

From

\[\ell_G=\frac{GE}{c^4},\]

\(\ell_G\propto E\).

41. Increasing Energy Moves the Two Reads Toward One Another

The quantum recurrence ruler decreases.

The gravitational geometry ruler increases.

Their product remains fixed.

42. The Crossover Occurs When

\[\ell_\omega=\ell_G.\]

Using

\(\ell_\omega\ell_G=\ell_P^2\),

one obtains

\(\ell_\omega=\ell_G=\ell_P\).

43. The Corresponding Energy Is the Planck Energy

Set

\[\frac{\hbar c}{E}=\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

Then:

\[E^2=\frac{\hbar c^5}{G}.\]

Thus:

\[E_P=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar c^5}{G}}.\]

44. R²D Does Not Need to Interpret This as a Smallest Possible Material Object

The equality does not by itself prove:

  • discrete Planck-sized spatial cells;
  • infinite density;
  • a singular primitive point;
  • or a new fundamental particle.

45. It Marks a Projection Crossover

The more conservative R²D interpretation is:

Quantum recurrence ruler = gravitational geometry ruler.

At this point the two complementary reads are no longer strongly scale separated.

46. This Gives the Planck Scale a Boundary-Relative Meaning

The Planck scale marks the regime in which neither the recurrence-only nor geometry-only projection can automatically be treated as independently complete.

47. The Full-Cycle Quantum Ruler Gives the Expected \(2\pi\)

Using

\(E=hf\),

define the full-cycle recurrence length

\[\lambda_f=\frac{c}{f}=\frac{hc}{E}.\]

48. Multiply by the Gravitational Ruler

\[\lambda_f\ell_G=\frac{hc}{E}\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

Therefore:

\[\lambda_f\ell_G=\frac{Gh}{c^3}.\]

Since

\[h=2\pi\hbar,\]

\[\lambda_f\ell_G=2\pi\ell_P^2.\]

49. The 2π Difference Is Coordinate Choice

The angular recurrence ruler uses

\[\omega\]

and

\[\hbar.\]

The full-cycle ruler uses

\[f\]

and

\[h.\]

The distinction should not be confused with a new physical scale.

50. \(c\) Is the Shared Propagation Calibration

The comparison between the two projection rulers also depends on

\[c.\]

On the quantum side it converts recurrence rate to a spatial recurrence ruler.

On the gravitational side it converts mass-energy to gravitational length.

Thus c participates in both physical projections.

51. The \(h/G\) Parallel Is Therefore Structural, Not Dimensional

It would be incorrect to say

\[h=G\]

or that they possess interchangeable units.

The claim is instead:

\[h,\hbar\longleftrightarrow G\]

as complementary physical place-value constants in opposite readability projections.

52. The Quantum Projection Has the Form

  1. Subordinate closure.
  2. Recursive replacement of subordinate geometry.
  3. Readable recurrence.
  4. Phase \(\phi\) and angular frequency \(\omega\).
  5. Energetic read \(E=\hbar\omega\).

53. The Gravitational Projection Has the Complementary Form

  1. Subordinate closure structure.

  2. Recursive replacement of subordinate recurrence detail.

  3. Readable enclosing distinction.

  4. Area \(\mathcal A\) and radius \(r\).

with gravitational length calibration

\[\ell_G=\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

54. Quantum and Gravity Are Therefore Not Primitive Opposites

They are complementary projections of a deeper paired relation.

One retains readable recurrence.

The other retains readable spatial distinction.

55. This Explains Why Their Conventional Mathematical Languages Look So Different

Quantum mechanics is naturally expressed through:

  • phase;
  • frequency;
  • complex recurrence;
  • amplitudes.

Gravity is naturally expressed through:

  • area;
  • radius;
  • metric geometry;
  • curvature.

R²D proposes that this difference reflects opposite readability horizons rather than unrelated primitive ontologies.

56. Thermodynamics Sits Between Them

Because both

\[\lambda\]

and

\[\nu\]

can remain jointly readable thermodynamically, thermodynamic systems can expose the recursive turbine architecture directly.

This is why molecular motors, heat engines, and statistical landscapes are unusually informative for R²D.

57. Principle — Recursive Replacement Precedes Physical Coordinates

Neither phase nor radius is primitive.

The count boundary determines what remains distinguishable before physical coordinates are assigned.

58. Principle — Quantum Phase Is the Coordinate of Surviving Recurrence

\[\text{closure recurrence}\to\phi.\]

59. Principle — Gravitational Radius Is the Coordinate of Surviving Spatial Distinction

\[\text{boundary relation}\to\mathcal A\to r.\]

60. Principle — \(h\) Calibrates Recurrence

\(E=hf\)

and

\(E=\hbar\omega\).

61. Principle — \(G\) Calibrates the Mass-Energy Geometry Read

\[\ell_G=\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

62. Principle — The Constants Do Not Cause Replacement

Recursive replacement occurs in the boundary-indexed count architecture.

The constants belong to later physical calibration.

63. Principle — Phase and Radius Are Complementary Projection Coordinates

\(\phi\leftrightarrow r\).

The correspondence is structural, not an equality.

64. Principle — The Quantum and Gravitational Rulers Are Reciprocal

\[\ell_\omega=\frac{\hbar c}{E},\]

\[\ell_G=\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]

Therefore:

\(\ell_\omega\ell_G=\ell_P^2\).

65. Principle — Planck Crossover Is Equality of Complementary Reads

\(\ell_\omega=\ell_G=\ell_P\).

This is the point at which the two opposite physical projection rulers cease to be strongly separated.

66. Logical Status

Canonical R²D

Boundary-defined closure has distinguishable and recurrent reads:

\[(\lambda_n,\nu_n).\]

Recursive replacement changes state identity across boundaries.

Established Readability Classification

Quantum:

\[\lambda\text{ unreadable},\qquad\nu\text{ readable}.\]

Gravity:

\[\lambda\text{ readable},\qquad\nu\text{ unreadable}.\]

Thermodynamics:

\[\lambda,\nu\text{ jointly readable}.\]

Quantum Physical Calibration

\[E=hf=\hbar\omega.\]

Gravitational Physical Calibration

\[\ell_G=\frac{GE}{c^4}.\]
