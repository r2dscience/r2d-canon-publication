Addendum 55 — Peer Compatibility Occurs Before Gravitational Interaction

Boundary Coupling, Mass–Area Projection, and the Causal Inversion of the Gravitational Many-Body Read

Addendum 55 — Peer Compatibility Occurs Before Gravitational Interaction

Abstract

Addendum 54 established the primitive content of R²D cosmology.

A cosmological boundary

\[B_\zeta\]

contains immediately subordinate peer boundaries

\[{B_{\zeta-1,\alpha} }\]

and counts their compatible joint realizations.

The peer boundaries exist as count domains before they are assigned physical quantities such as mass, radius, energy density, gravitational field, or spacetime coordinates.

This leaves the next problem:

How does an already-defined peer-boundary relation become readable as gravity?

R²D cannot answer this by placing mass or G back into the primitive compatibility relation.

That would replace the top-down R²D causal architecture with the bottom-up gravitational representation before the transition between them had been identified.

The bridge must therefore be explicit.

For peer boundary

\[B_{\zeta-1,\alpha}\]

let

\[\mu_\alpha\]

denote a dimensionless orientation-even coupling read.

A physical mass calibration assigns

\[m_\alpha=M_{\mathrm{cal}}\mu_\alpha.\]

Mass does not create the peer boundary.

It is a dimensional physical read of a coupling already associated with that boundary.

Addendum 52 then supplies the gravitational spatial calibration

\[\ell_{G,\alpha}=\frac{Gm_\alpha}{c^2}\]

Define

\[L_{\mathrm{cal}}=\frac{GM_{\mathrm{cal}}}{c^2}\]

Then

\[\ell_{G,\alpha} = L_{\mathrm{cal}} \mu_\alpha\]

The same peer relation can therefore be read successively as

\[B_{\zeta-1,\alpha}\]

↓

\[\mu_\alpha\]

↓

\[m_\alpha\xrightarrow{G/c^2}\ell_{G,\alpha}\]

The enclosing peer relation must also acquire a spatial read.

Following the gravitational architecture already developed in R²D, the enclosing boundary projects first onto area,

\[B_G \to A_G\]

and the corresponding areal radius is defined from that area,

\[r_A=\sqrt{\frac{A_G}{4\pi}}\]

Radius is therefore not the primitive container within which the peer relation occurs.

It is a geometric coordinate derived from the projected boundary area.

Once both physical projections exist, the conventional gravitational relation can be written.

For a spherically symmetric weak-field read,

\[g=\frac{Gm}{r_A^2}\]

Using

\[A_G = 4\pi r_A^2\]

this is

\[gA_G = 4\pi Gm\]

With

\[\ell_G=\frac{Gm}{c^2}\]

the same relation becomes

\[gA_G = 4\pi c^2 \ell_G\]

This equation combines two downstream gravitational coordinates:

• the gravitational source place value ℓG ; • the projected enclosing area AG .

Neither is the primitive source of peer compatibility.

The primitive R²D causal architecture remains

Bζ → compatible joint realization of its peers.

After gravitational projection, that same organization can be represented as

mass-energy source → gravitational field or geometry → subordinate trajectory.

Thus gravitational projection reverses the apparent direction of causal explanation.

The conventional bottom-up equation can remain quantitatively valid while the primitive R²D causal relation remains top-down.

This distinction becomes especially important for many-body gravity.

Conventional Newtonian mechanics writes

\[\ddot{\mathbf r}_\alpha=-G\sum_{\beta\ne\alpha}m_\beta\frac{\mathbf r_\alpha-\mathbf r_\beta}{|\mathbf r_\alpha-\mathbf r_\beta|^3}\]

The equation treats the subordinate masses as sources of one another’s accelerations.

R²D begins one layer earlier.

The peers are jointly constrained because they participate in one enclosing compatible realization:

\[B_\zeta\longrightarrow\{B_{\zeta-1,1},B_{\zeta-1,2},\ldots\}_{\mathrm{compatible}}\]

The pairwise gravitational interaction is therefore a candidate physical representation of an already-defined joint peer compatibility.

It need not be the primitive cause of that compatibility.

The same distinction extends to general relativity.

Einstein’s field equation,

\[G_{\mu\nu}[g]=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu}\]

relates an energetic source read

\[T_{\mu\nu}\]

to a geometric curvature read

\[G_{\mu\nu}\]

R²D does not need to assign primitive causal priority to either side.

Instead, define a gravitational projection

\[\Pi_G:\Omega_\zeta\to\Gamma_G.\]

where

\[\Omega_\zeta\]

is the compatible joint peer-realization domain and

\[\Gamma_G\]

is the corresponding gravitational physical state domain.

A projected compatible realization may possess both a source read and a geometric read,

\[\Pi_G(\mu_{\zeta,r})=\bigl(T_{\mu\nu}^{(r)},g_{\mu\nu}^{(r)}\bigr)\]

For a general-relativistic projection, physically admissible image pairs satisfy

\[G_{\mu\nu}[g^{(r)}]=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu}^{(r)}\]

Einstein’s equation can therefore be read as a compatibility condition within the gravitational image of a prior peer-boundary relation.

This is not yet a derivation of the Einstein field equation from R²D.

It specifies where such a derivation would have to occur.

The central claim is therefore:

And hence:

peer compatibility occurs before gravitational interaction.

\[\mu_\alpha \to m_\alpha\]

not

\[m_\alpha \to \mu_\alpha\]

The physical mass coordinate reads an already-defined coupling relation.

\[\ell_{G,\alpha} = L_{\mathrm{cal}} \mu_\alpha\]
