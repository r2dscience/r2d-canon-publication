Addendum 81 — Repeated Subordinate Closure Occurs Before Quantum Interference

Atmospheric Deprojection, Recursive Replacement, and the Double-Slit Distribution

Abstract

Quantum interference is conventionally represented by the coherent addition of complex amplitudes,

\[ \psi=\psi_a+\psi_b, \]

followed by the realization measure

\[|\psi|^2\]

That representation is extraordinarily successful.

R2D asks what physical architecture must exist before such a phase representation is available.

The answer begins with a relation already directly visible in ordinary thermodynamics.

An atmosphere possesses a boundary-defined radial possibility and occupancy structure. A subordinate molecule repeatedly moves through that structure. Over sufficiently many recurrent realizations, the realized occupancy of one molecule can reproduce the larger atmospheric distribution.

The molecule does not create that distribution.

It realizes it.

Thus:

enclosing organization → subordinate recurrence → enclosing occupancy readout.

The atmospheric example is not mysterious because the subordinate realizer and the enclosing distribution can both be made readable.

Quantum interference has the same recursive architecture on the opposite side of a readability horizon.

At the quantum boundary, subordinate geometric state identity has already undergone recursive replacement. The subordinate realization can continue to recur, but it cannot be jointly read as the same persistent spatial object together with the enclosing quantum distribution.

Addendum 3 established the quantum readability condition:

\[ (\lambda,\nu)_Q=(\mathrm{unreadable},\mathrm{readable}). \]

What survives is recurrence.

Repeated subordinate closure can therefore realize a slower enclosing quantum relation even though the subordinate realizing trajectory is no longer available as a physical read.

The recursive clock gives

\[ f_Q=\rho_{Q|s}f_s,\qquad0<\rho_{Q|s}<1, \]

so subordinate realization is faster than the enclosing quantum recurrence:

\[ f_s>f_Q\]

In a strongly separated regime,

\[\frac{f_s}{f_Q}=\frac{1}{\rho_{Q\mid s}}\gg1\]

Thus one readable enclosing occurrence can be recursively realized through many faster subordinate closures.

The double-slit apparatus constrains the compatible enclosing relation.

If the slit alternatives do not become separately readable outcomes, their subordinate distinction does not survive recursive replacement as a path label.

The surviving relation is recurrent.

Only then is it represented through phase.

Thus the logical order is

enclosing compatibility→repeated subordinate closure→recursive replacement→coherent recurrence→phase representation→detector occupancy.

The conventional quantum law follows downstream.

For two unresolved recurrence contributions,

\[\psi_a(x)=A_a(x)e^{i\phi_a(x)}\]

\[\psi_b(x)=A_b(x)e^{i\phi_b(x)}\]

and

\[\psi_Q(x)=\psi_a(x)+\psi_b(x)\]

The detector realization measure is then

\[ \mu_D(x)=|\psi_Q(x)|^2, \]

giving

\[ \mu_D(x)=|\psi_a|^2+|\psi_b|^2+2A_aA_b\cos(\phi_b-\phi_a). \]

The interference term is therefore not taken as primitive evidence that one persistent particle follows two classical trajectories.

It is the phase-coordinate read of an enclosing recurrence relation whose subordinate realizing geometry is no longer jointly readable.

Which-path measurement changes the architecture.

If an enclosing marker boundary makes the alternatives distinguishable,

\[ M_a\ne M_b, \]

the count domain changes.

The conventional joint representation becomes

\[ |\Psi(x)\rangle=\psi_a(x)|M_a\rangle+\psi_b(x)|M_b\rangle, \]

and the local detector distribution is

\[\mu_D(x)=|\psi_a|^2+|\psi_b|^2+2\operatorname{Re}\!\left[\langle M_a|M_b\rangle\psi_a^*\psi_b\right]\]

The overlap

\[\langle M_a|M_b\rangle\]

then quantifies how completely the enclosing marker boundary retains the subordinate distinction.

Interference survives when the distinction remains unresolved.

It disappears locally when the distinction becomes fully readable.

The central R2D statement is:

quantum interference is the recurrence-side read of an enclosing distribution realized through faster subordinate closure after subordinate geometric state identity has crossed the readability horizon. The same recursive architecture is directly deprojectable in an atmosphere, where one molecule can repeatedly realize the larger radial occupancy landscape. In the quantum case the subordinate realizer is not jointly readable, while coherent recurrence survives and is represented through phase. The wavefunction therefore describes the surviving recurrence relation; the detector particle is a completed enclosing count. Neither needs to be projected backward as a primitive hidden trajectory.

And hence:

repeated subordinate closure occurs before quantum interference.

1. Begin With an Ordinary Atmosphere

Consider an atmospheric boundary

\[ B_A\]

Let

\[ i\]

index radial or height macrostates within that boundary.

The atmosphere defines a physically realized occupancy landscape

\[\{\mu_{A,i}\}\]

2. The Atmospheric Distribution Belongs to the Atmospheric Boundary

The distribution is not primitively assigned to one oxygen molecule.

It describes the compatible realization of the atmosphere as a whole.

Thus:

\[ B_A\]

3. A Molecule Is a Subordinate Realizer

Now descend to a subordinate molecular boundary

\[ B_m\]

At that boundary, an individual molecule possesses readable local motion.

It may rise, fall, collide, reverse, and recur through many atmospheric radial states.

4. One Molecule Can Repeatedly Realize the Larger Distribution

Let

\[ n_i(N_C)\]

be the number of realized visits of one molecule to radial state i over NC sufficiently mixing recurrent realizations.

Define its realized occupancy fraction

\[\widetilde\mu_i(N_C)=\frac{n_i(N_C)}{\sum_j n_j(N_C)}\]

Under the usual long-run statistical conditions,

\[\widetilde\mu_i(N_C)\to\mu_{A,i}\]

5. This Does Not Mean the Molecule Created the Atmospheric Landscape

The causal order is not

molecular wandering → atmospheric distribution.

R2D reverses the attribution:

atmospheric compatibility → molecular realization.

6. The Time Average Reveals the Landscape

Repeated molecular realization can reproduce the enclosing occupancy relation.

The time average is therefore evidence of recursive realization.

It is not the primitive source of the distribution.

7. The Atmospheric Example Is Deprojectable

The atmosphere is unusually useful because both levels can be observed.

At BA , one reads:

radial occupancy.

At Bm , one can in principle read:

molecular trajectory and recurrence.

8. Both Sides of the Recursion Are Therefore Comparatively Accessible

The atmosphere lies near the jointly readable thermodynamic regime:

\[(\lambda,\nu)=(R,R)\]

Spatial distinction and subordinate recurrence can both remain physically accessible.

9. The Atmosphere Exposes the Recursive Clock

Subordinate molecular motion can be extremely rapid.

The enclosing atmospheric distribution can remain nearly stationary over the same physical interval.

Thus:

\[ f_m\gg f_A\]

for an appropriate comparison of native recurrence scales.

10. Faster Subordinate Realization Does Not Cause the Enclosing Relation

The molecule is necessary for local realization.

It does not independently determine the atmospheric possibility landscape.

This is the same R2D turbine ordering seen in other thermodynamic systems:

enclosing organization → subordinate recurrence → enclosing readout.

11. Probability Is a Later Normalization

Once realized occupancies are counted, they may be normalized.

Thus:

possible count → realized occupancy → normalized probability.

12. The Atmospheric Projection Error Is to Assign the Landscape Downward

Because one molecule can reproduce the distribution over time, it is tempting to say:

the molecule possesses the probability distribution.

R2D instead says:

the atmospheric boundary defines the distribution.

The molecule repeatedly realizes it.

13. Quantum Interference Repeats the Same Attribution Problem

In a double-slit experiment, repeated localized detector events build an extended distribution.

Conventional language then asks how each individual particle carried or traversed that distribution.

R2D asks first whether the distribution has again been projected downward onto the counted object.

\[ B_s\]

denote the relevant subordinate realization boundary,

\[ B_Q\]

the coherent quantum/apparatus boundary,

\[ B_D\]

the detector boundary.

At Bs , the subordinate closure possesses its own state distinctions.

They are not automatically the state distinctions of BQ .

16. Recursive Replacement Occurs Before the Quantum Read

When the count boundary changes,

\[ B_s\to B_Q\]

the subordinate state identity is replaced.

Thus:

subordinate state≠quantum-boundary state.

17. The Subordinate Path Need Not Survive as a Hidden Primitive Object

R2D does not interpret recursive replacement as ordinary coarse-graining.

The subordinate path is not merely blurred while remaining the primitive state underneath.

Its state identity belongs to a different count domain.

18. Addendum 3 Defines the Quantum Readability Condition

At the quantum horizon,

\[\lambda_s\to U\]

while

\[\nu_Q\to R\]

The subordinate continuous geometry is not jointly readable.

Recurrence remains readable.

19. The Missing Geometry Has Not Been Destroyed

Unreadability is not nonrealization.

Subordinate closure may continue at its own boundary.

What is lost is its direct readability as the same spatial state at the enclosing quantum boundary.

20. The Quantum Mystery Therefore Begins With One-Sided Readability

In the atmosphere one can observe both:

subordinate realizer + enclosing distribution.

At the quantum boundary one reads:

enclosing recurrence/distribution,

without jointly recovering the subordinate geometric realization.

21. The Recursive Clock Applies Before Quantum Phase

Let

\[\rho_{Q\mid s}=\frac{N_\chi^{(Q)}}{N_C^{(s)}}\]

where \(N_C^{(s)}\) counts subordinate closures associated with one enclosing occurrence and \(N_\chi^{(Q)}\) counts the enclosing occurrence.

For a nontrivial recursive relation,

\[0<\rho_{Q\mid s}<1\]

22. For One Enclosing Quantum Occurrence

If

\[ N_\chi^{(Q)}=1\]

then the subordinate realization count is schematically

\[ N_C^{(s\mid Q)}\sim\frac{1}{\rho_{Q\mid s}}\]

23. A Strongly Separated Regime Contains Many Subordinate Closures

When

\[\rho_{Q\mid s}\ll1\]

then

\[ N_C^{(s\mid Q)}\gg1\]

Thus one readable enclosing quantum occurrence can be realized through many subordinate closures.

24. Physical Clock Calibration Gives the Same Ordering

When both recurrence classes acquire physical frequency reads,

\[ f_Q=\rho_{Q\mid s}f_s\]

Hence:

\[ f_s=\frac{f_Q}{\rho_{Q\mid s}}\]

25. Therefore

\[ f_s>f_Q\]

And for strong scale separation,

\[ f_s\gg f_Q\]

27. The Difference Is Readability
