Abstract

Addendum 58 decomposed the physical Hubble rate into two logically distinct quantities:

\[ H=f_cH_{\zeta\mid c}.\]

where

\[ f_c=\frac{dN_C^{(c)}}{dt}\]

is the physical clock calibration and

\[\mathcal H_{\zeta\mid c}=\frac12\frac{d\ln A_\zeta}{dN_C^{(c)}}\]

is dimensionless cosmological progress per completed clock closure.

This isolated the remaining problem.

The frozen R²D canon supplies a candidate answer, but not through a direct identification of cosmological asymmetry with physical rate.

R²D separates several count relations that must remain distinct.

Local directional asymmetry biases how realized occurrence is expressed as occupancy. It does not simply replace the native occurrence structure with a physical rate law.

The R²D law addresses a different question.

It relates the recurrence of a representative subordinate closure to recurrence of the enclosing distinguishable occurrence through which that closure is counted.

Consider an enclosing occurrence at boundary Bn+1 associated with readable transition

\[\chi_{n+1}:j\to k\]

Let

\[ C_{n,a}\]

be a representative peer closure through which this enclosing occurrence is locally realized.

Within one common recurrence-comparison domain, define

\[ N_C^{(n,a)}\]

as the number of completed recurrences of the representative peer closure and

\[ N_\chi^{(n+1)}\]

as the number of completed realizations of the enclosing transition class.

The adjacent recurrence ratio is

\[\rho_{n+1\mid n,a}=\frac{N_\chi^{(n+1)}}{N_C^{(n,a)}}\]

The frozen R²D law is

\[\Delta A_{n,a,C}=C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1}\rho_{n+1\mid n,a}\]

where

\[\Delta A_{n,a,C}=\Delta P_{n,a,C}-\Delta R_{n,a,C}\]

is the net asymmetry accumulated around the representative subordinate closure,

\[\lambda_{n+1}\]

is the distinguishability of the enclosing transition, and

\[ C_{n+1}\]

is the total receiving constraint.

Equivalently,

\[\Delta A_{n,a,C}N_C^{(n,a)}=C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1}N_\chi^{(n+1)}\]

The canon interprets this as an invariant recurrence-weighted relation between two boundary-specific readings of one recursively organized occurrence. It assigns no physical time unit to either recurrence count.

Therefore,

\[\rho_{n+1\mid n,a}=\frac{\Delta A_{n,a,C}}{C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1}}\]

This ratio is a dimensionless recursive throughput.

It measures how many enclosing transition recurrences are realized per representative subordinate closure recurrence within the specified comparison domain.

Apply this law to the cosmological bridge

\[ B_{\zeta-1,a}\to B_\zeta\]

Let

\[\chi_\zeta:i\to k\]

be one readable cosmological transition class.

Then

\[\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}=\frac{N_\chi^\zeta}{N_C^{(a)}}=\frac{\Delta A_{\zeta-1,a,C}}{C_\zeta\lambda_\zeta}\]

The causal ordering remains top-down.

The equation does not mean that subordinate closures generate the cosmological occurrence.

The enclosing occurrence defines the compatible peer closures through which it is realized.

The recurrence ratio measures the throughput of that realization.

Now assign the cosmological transition its geometric area read.

For one occurrence

\[\chi_\zeta:i\to k\]

define

\[\delta_{A,\chi}=\frac12\ln\frac{A_{\zeta,k}}{A_{\zeta,i}}\]

Under the isotropic scale-factor mapping of Addendum 56,

\[\delta_{A,\chi}=\ln\frac{a_k}{a_i}\]

This is the logarithmic linear-scale step associated with one enclosing cosmological occurrence.

Suppose, first, that one coherent transition class dominates the comparison domain and that each recurrence of that class carries the same geometric step.

Then

\[\frac12\Delta\ln A_\zeta=\delta_{A,\chi}N_\chi^\zeta\]

\[\mathcal H_{\zeta\mid a}=\delta_{A,\chi}\frac{N_\chi^\zeta}{N_C^{(a)}}\]

Therefore,

\[\mathcal H_{\zeta\mid a}=\delta_{A,\chi}\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}\]

Using the R²D law,

\[\mathcal H_{\zeta\mid a}=\delta_{A,\chi}\frac{\Delta A_{\zeta-1,a,C}}{C_\zeta\lambda_\zeta}\]

This is a dimensionless recursive-throughput relation.

No physical time appears.

No mass appears.

No G appears.

No density appears.

No Friedmann equation appears.

\[ f_a\]

\[ H=f_a\delta_{A,\chi}\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}.\]

\[ H=f_a\delta_{A,\chi}\frac{\Delta A_{\zeta-1,a,C}}{C_\zeta\lambda_\zeta}\]

\(f_a\) = physical temporal calibration

\(\delta_{A,\chi}\) = geometric step per cosmological occurrence

and

\(\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}\) = recursive occurrence throughput

The R²D law determines the third relation.

\(A_\zeta=P_\zeta-R_\zeta\) biases which cosmological structural transition is realized.

\[\Delta A_\zeta-1,a,C\]

\(A_\zeta\to\) branch selection

\(\Delta A_{\zeta-1,a,C}\to\) recurrence-weighted realization geometry

The second arrow denotes the role of the boundary-specific count relation, not bottom-up mechanical causation.

The enclosing occurrence remains causally prior to the compatible subordinate closures.

The mature physical rate can now be compared directly with the Friedmann magnitude.
