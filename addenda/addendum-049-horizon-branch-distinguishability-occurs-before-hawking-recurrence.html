Addendum 49 — Horizon-Branch Distinguishability Occurs Before Hawking Recurrence

Abstract

Addendum 48 established a stationary Kerr–Newman horizon count domain at fixed physical mass.

Its rotational and charge macrostates are physically labeled by

\[j=\frac J\hbar\]

and

\[q=\frac Q{q_P}\]

within the admissible domain

\[j^2+j_Mq^2\le j_M^2\]

where

\[j_M=\frac{GM^2}{\hbar c}\]

The Kerr–Newman residual coordinate is

\[\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}=\sqrt{j_M^2-j^2-j_Mq^2}\]

The physical horizon state projects onto two geometric branches,

\[x_\pm=j_M\pm\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

with corresponding area-calibrated logarithmic multiplicity coordinates

\[S_\pm=\ln W_\pm=2\pi(j_M\pm\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}})-\pi q^2\]

Their difference is therefore

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}\equiv S_+-S_-=4\pi\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}.\]

Since

\[S_\pm = \ln W_\pm\]

this may also be written

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}=\ln\frac{W_+}{W_-}\]

The same projected branch distinction possesses a radial read.

Because

\[x=\frac{Mc}{\hbar}r\]

the radial separation of the two horizon branches is

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}\equiv r_+-r_-=\frac{2\hbar}{Mc}\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

Thus

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}=\frac{2\pi Mc}{\hbar}\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}\]

This equality is specific to the Kerr–Newman physical projection.

It does not identify entropy with radius, circumference, or primitive R²D distinguishability.

It says that the same projected outer–inner branch separation acquires both a radial read and an area/ multiplicity read.

The outer-horizon surface gravity is

\[\kappa_H=\frac{c^2(r_+-r_-)}{2(r_+^2+a^2)}\]

Therefore the Hawking phase-frequency read is

\[\omega_H=\frac{\kappa_H}{2\pi c}=\frac{c(r_+-r_-)}{4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)}\]

Using

\[S_+=\frac{\pi(r_+^2+a^2)}{\ell_P^2}\]

this becomes

\[\omega_H=\frac{c\,\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}}{4\ell_P^2S_+}\]

Equivalently,

\[\frac{\hbar\omega_H}{Mc^2}=\frac{S_+-S_-}{8\pi j_MS_+}\]

Define the normalized branch multiplicity gap

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}}\equiv\frac{S_+-S_-}{S_+}=1-\frac{S_-}{S_+}\]

Then

\[\frac{\hbar\omega_H}{Mc^2}=\frac{\delta_{\mathrm{br}}}{8\pi j_M}\]

At fixed mass,

\[\omega_H\propto\delta_{\mathrm{br}}.\]

The Hawking clock therefore does not read total horizon multiplicity.

It reads the remaining outer–inner branch distinction under the Kerr–Newman thermal calibration.

At Schwarzschild,

\[S_- = 0\]

so

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}} = 1\]

At extremality,

\[\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}} = 0\]

and therefore

\[r_+ = r_-\]

\[S_+ = S_-\]

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}} = 0\]

Consequently,

\[\omega_H = 0\]

But the horizon boundary has not disappeared.

At extremality,

\[S_+ > 0\]

and nontrivial rotational and/or charge organization remains.

Addendum 48 also established that the stationary macrostate family is mapped to the differential stall relation

\[dP_{\mathrm{KN}} = dR_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

so

\[dA_{\mathrm{KN}} = 0\]

throughout the stationary family, not only at extremality.

Therefore the vanishing of Hawking recurrence cannot be identified with:

\[S_+\]

\[R_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

\[P_{\mathrm{KN}} - R_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

or the existence of the gravitational horizon itself.

What vanishes is the projected branch distinction.

The causal order is therefore

horizon boundary

↓

(j, q) horizon state

↓

outer/inner branch projection

↓

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}} , \Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}\]

↓

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}}\]

↓

\[\omega_H\]

↓

\[T_H\]

Temperature comes last.

Thus:

Hawking recurrence does not sustain the horizon.

Rather,

Hawking recurrence is a thermal clock read conditioned by a branch distinction already sustained by

Therefore:

horizon-branch distinguishability occurs before Hawking recurrence.

1. Begin With the Horizon Boundary, Not Its Temperature

R²D begins by asking which boundary owns the state distinctions.

Let

\[B_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

denote the fixed-mass stationary Kerr–Newman horizon count boundary.

Its physical macrostate is specified by the rotational and charge coordinates

\[(j, q)\]

The horizon does not become a count boundary because it possesses a Hawking temperature.

Its state domain is defined first.

Thermal recurrence is a later physical read.

2. Addendum 48 Established the Native Horizon State

At fixed mass,

\[j_M=\frac{GM^2}{\hbar c}\]

The admissible state domain is

\[j^2+j_Mq^2\le j_M^2\]

The residual geometric coordinate is

\[\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}=\sqrt{j_M^2-j^2-j_Mq^2}\]

The boundary exists throughout the nonextremal and extremal state domain.

3. Extremality Is Not the Stationary Stall Condition

Addendum 48 mapped the stationary fixed-mass family to

\[dP_{\mathrm{KN}} = dR_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

Thus

\[dA_{\mathrm{KN}} = 0\]

throughout that stationary manifold.

Extremality, by contrast, is

\[\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}} = 0\]

Therefore,

stationary differential stall \(\\ne\) extremality.

This distinction is essential.

4. The Outer and Inner Horizons Are Projected Branches

The phase-normalized horizon polynomial produces

\[x_\pm=j_M\pm\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

These are two roots belonging to the same physically specified Kerr–Newman horizon state.

They should not be treated as two independent primitive R²D states.

They are two geometric branches of one boundary-owned physical relation.

5. The Branches Have Radial Separation

Since

\[x=\frac{Mc}{\hbar}r\]

we have

\[r_\pm=\frac\hbar{Mc}x_\pm\]

Therefore,

\[r_+-r_-=\frac\hbar{Mc}(x_+-x_-)\]

But

\[x_+ - x_- = 2\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

Hence,

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}=r_+-r_-=\frac{2\hbar}{Mc}\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

6. The Same Branch Separation Has an Area/Multiplicity Read

The horizon area calibration gives

\[S_\pm=\ln W_\pm=\frac{A_\pm}{4\ell_P^2}\]

For Kerr–Newman,

\[S_\pm=2\pi(j_M\pm\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}})-\pi q^2\]

Subtracting,

\[S_+ - S_- = 4\pi\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

Define

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}} \equiv S_+ - S_-\]

Then

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}} = 4\pi\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

7. The Multiplicity Gap Is a Logarithmic Ratio

Because

\[S_+ = \ln W_+\]

and

\[S_- = \ln W_-\]

we have

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}} = \ln W_+ - \ln W_-\]

Therefore,

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}=\ln\frac{W_+}{W_-}\]

The branch gap is therefore more than a geometric area difference.

It is a logarithmic multiplicity ratio under the horizon-area calibration.

8. Radial and Multiplicity Reads Must Not Be Collapsed

From

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}=\frac{2\hbar}{Mc}\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

and

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}} = 4\pi\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

we obtain

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}=\frac{2\pi Mc}{\hbar}\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}\]

This relation belongs to the Kerr–Newman physical projection.

It does not imply

\[S = \lambda\]

Nor does it imply

entropy = radius.

The two quantities are distinct physical reads of the same projected branch splitting.

9. This Is Consistent With Area Before Radius

The boundary multiplicities remain area calibrated:

\[S_\pm=\frac{A_\pm}{4\ell_P^2}\]

The branch separation also possesses a radial physical read,

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}\]

Thus:

boundary count → area/multiplicity read,

while

branch separation → radial distinguishability read.

No circumferential entropy interpretation is required.

10. Equal Multiplicity Alone Would Not Prove Branch Identity

In general,

\[W_1 = W_2\]

does not require two states or branches to be identical.

Equal degeneracy can occur for distinguishable states.

Kerr–Newman extremality supplies additional information.

At extremality,

\[r_+ = r_-\]

at the same time that

\[W_+ = W_-\]

Thus both the geometric branch separation and the multiplicity gap vanish together.

11. Extremality Closes the Radial Branch Gap

The normalized admissible state domain is

\[\chi_J^2 + \chi_Q^2 \le 1\]

Extremality is

\[\chi_J^2 + \chi_Q^2 = 1\]

Therefore,

\[\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}} = 0\]

Hence,

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}} = 0\]

The two horizon radii coalesce.

12. Extremality Also Closes the Multiplicity Gap

Because

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}} = 4\pi\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

extremality gives

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}} = 0\]

Thus,

\[\ln\frac{W_+}{W_-}=0\]

Therefore,

\[W_+ = W_-\]

The outer–inner multiplicity distinction has closed.

13. The Horizon Boundary Itself Remains

At extremality,

\[S_+ = S_-\]

but

\[S_+ > 0\]

Therefore,

\[W_+ = e^{S_+} > 1\]

The loss of outer–inner branch distinction is not disappearance of the horizon multiplicity landscape.

Thus,

branch closure \(\\ne\) horizon boundary collapse

14. Retained Rotational and/or Charge Organization Also Remains

A nontrivial extremal Kerr–Newman horizon satisfies

\[\chi_J^2 + \chi_Q^2 = 1\]

Thus rotation, charge, or both remain nonzero.

The extremal state therefore simultaneously has

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}} = 0\]

and

retained horizon organization \(\\ne\) 0.

Loss of branch distinction is not loss of retention.

15. Now Introduce the Thermal Clock

Only after the horizon state and its branch structure have been defined do we introduce the Hawking phase-frequency read.

Define

\[\omega_H\equiv\frac{k_BT_H}{\hbar}\]

Equivalently,

\[\omega_H=\frac{\kappa_H}{2\pi c}\]

Temperature and frequency are therefore later physical calibrations.

16. The Kerr–Newman Surface Gravity Contains the Branch Separation Explicitly

For the outer Kerr–Newman horizon,

\[\kappa_H=\frac{c^2(r_+-r_-)}{2(r_+^2+a^2)}\]

Therefore,

\[\omega_H=\frac{c(r_+-r_-)}{4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)}\]

Using

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}} = r_+ - r_-\]

we obtain

\[\omega_H=\frac{c\,\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}}{4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)}\]

The branch separation is already explicit before temperature is assigned.

17. Replace the Area Factor With Horizon Multiplicity

For the outer horizon,

\[S_+=\frac{\pi(r_+^2+a^2)}{\ell_P^2}\]

Therefore,

\[r_+^2+a^2=\frac{\ell_P^2S_+}{\pi}\]

Substituting gives

\[\omega_H=\frac{c\,\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}}{4\ell_P^2S_+}\]

This is a direct physical branch-gap representation of the Hawking clock.

18. Hawking Recurrence Is Not Set by Total Multiplicity Alone

The equation

\[\omega_H=\frac{c\,\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}}{4\ell_P^2S_+}\]

contains both

\[S_+\]

and

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}\]

Thus two horizons with the same outer multiplicity but different branch separation would not possess the same Hawking read.

The clock depends on the remaining branch distinction.

19. Rewrite the Thermal Clock Using the Multiplicity Gap

From

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}=\frac{2\pi Mc}{\hbar}\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}\]

we have

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}}=\frac\hbar{2\pi Mc}\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}\]

Insert this into the Hawking relation:

\[\omega_H=\frac c{4\ell_P^2S_+}\frac\hbar{2\pi Mc}\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}\]

Using

\[\ell_P^2=\frac{G\hbar}{c^3}\]

we obtain

\[\frac{\hbar\omega_H}{Mc^2}=\frac{\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}}{8\pi j_MS_+}\]

20. Write the Result Directly in Multiplicities

Since

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}}=\ln\frac{W_+}{W_-}\]

and

\[S_+ = \ln W_+\]

we obtain

\[\frac{\hbar\omega_H}{Mc^2}=\frac1{8\pi j_M}\frac{\ln(W_+/W_-)}{\ln W_+}\]

The Hawking clock is therefore tied directly to a relative horizon multiplicity distinction.

21. Define the Normalized Branch Multiplicity Gap

Define

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}}\equiv\frac{S_+-S_-}{S_+}\]

Equivalently,

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}}=1-\frac{S_-}{S_+}\]

And, using multiplicities,

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}}=\frac{\ln(W_+/W_-)}{\ln W_+}\]

This quantity compares the branch multiplicity gap with the outer-horizon multiplicity scale.

22. The Normalized Gap Is Bounded

For the Kerr–Newman family considered here,

\[0 \le S_- \le S_+\]

Therefore,

\[0 \le \delta_{\mathrm{br}} \le 1\]

The two endpoints have immediate physical meanings.

23. Hawking Recurrence Becomes Linear in the Normalized Gap

The thermal bridge becomes

\[\frac{\hbar\omega_H}{Mc^2}=\frac{\delta_{\mathrm{br}}}{8\pi j_M}\]

At fixed mass,

\[j_M\]

is fixed.

Therefore,

\[\omega_H\propto\delta_{\mathrm{br}}.\]

This is the central physical result.

24. Schwarzschild Is the Maximally Separated Endpoint

For Schwarzschild,

\[j = 0, q = 0\]

Then

\[\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}} = j_M\]

The branch counts become

\[S_+ = 4\pi j_M\]

and

\[S_- = 0\]

Therefore,

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}} = 1\]

Within this normalized family, the branch distinction is maximal.

25. The Schwarzschild Hawking Clock Follows

At

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}} = 1\]

the thermal bridge becomes

\[\frac{\hbar\omega_{\mathrm{Schw}}}{Mc^2}=\frac1{8\pi j_M}\]

Therefore,

\[\omega_{\mathrm{Schw}}=\frac{c^3}{8\pi GM}\]

This is the standard Schwarzschild Hawking phase-frequency read.

26. Rotation and Charge Reduce the Branch Gap

Moving away from the Schwarzschild point at fixed mass increases

\[j^2 + j_M q^2\]

Therefore,

\[\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

decreases.

Since

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}} = 4\pi\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

the outer–inner multiplicity gap decreases.

The Hawking clock decreases with it.

27. The Thermal Clock Approaches Zero at Extremality

At extremality,

\[\Delta_{\mathrm{KN}} = 0\]

Therefore,

\[\Delta r_{\mathrm{br}} = 0\]

\[\Delta S_{\mathrm{br}} = 0\]

and

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}} = 0\]

Hence,

\[\omega_H = 0\]

And therefore,

\[T_H = 0\]

within the standard stationary semiclassical Kerr–Newman calibration.

28. Total Horizon Multiplicity Does Not Vanish

At the same extremal point,

\[S_+ > 0\]

Thus,

\[\omega_H=0\quad\text{while}\quad S_+>0\]

Therefore,

\[\omega_H \ne S_+\]

Hawking recurrence is not a direct read of the amount of horizon multiplicity.

29. Retention Does Not Vanish

Addendum 48 defined the cumulative retention read

\[R_{\mathrm{KN}} = S_{\mathrm{Schw}} - S_+\]

At nontrivial extremality this quantity remains nonzero.

Yet

\[\omega_H = 0\]

Therefore,

\[\omega_H \ne R_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

Hawking recurrence is not the retained horizon coordinate.

30. Stationary Asymmetry Does Not Track Hawking Recurrence

Addendum 48 mapped the stationary family to

\[dP_{\mathrm{KN}} = dR_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

Thus

\[dA_{\mathrm{KN}} = 0\]

throughout the stationary family.

But

\[\omega_H\]

varies continuously from the Schwarzschild value to zero.

Therefore Hawking recurrence cannot be the physical read of the stationary R²D asymmetry.

Thus,

\[\omega_H \ne A_{\mathrm{KN}}\]

31. Extremality Is Not “More Stalled” Than Schwarzschild

Because

\[dA_{\mathrm{KN}} = 0\]

already characterizes the stationary family under the Addendum 48 mapping, extremality is not the point at which stall is newly achieved.

What changes at extremality is

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}}\]

It reaches zero.

Thus:

extremality = branch closure,

not

extremality = onset of stationary stall.

32. The Horizon Boundary Does Not Depend on Hawking Recurrence for Its Existence

If Hawking recurrence constituted the horizon boundary itself, then

\[\omega_H = 0\]

would imply disappearance of that boundary.

But at extremality,

\[S_+ > 0\]

and retained charge and/or rotation remains.

Therefore,

Hawking recurrence does not sustain the horizon boundary.

The boundary sustains the distinction that the Hawking clock reads.

33. The Causal Inversion Is Therefore Clear

The conventional presentation often begins with surface gravity,

\[\kappa_H\]

and derives temperature.

R²D pushes the interpretation earlier.

The physical state defines two projected branches.

Those branches possess a radial and multiplicity separation.

That separation then appears in surface gravity.

Thus the explanatory ordering is

branch distinction → \(\\kappa_H\) → \(\\omega_H\) → \(T_H\) .

Not the reverse.

34. Surface Gravity Is a Later Geometric Read

Since

\[\omega_H=\frac{\kappa_H}{2\pi c}\]

and

\[\omega_H \propto \delta_{\mathrm{br}}\]

at fixed mass, we have

\[\kappa_H \propto \delta_{\mathrm{br}}\]

Thus surface gravity itself is a physical geometric read conditioned by branch separation.

It does not define the primitive horizon state.

35. Hawking Temperature Comes Later Still

Using

\[k_B T_H = \hbar\omega_H\]

we obtain

\[\frac{k_BT_H}{Mc^2}=\frac{\delta_{\mathrm{br}}}{8\pi j_M}\]

Thus the ordering is

\[\delta_{\mathrm{br}} \to \omega_H \to T_H\]

The temperature is the final thermal dimensionalization.

36. Hawking Recurrence Is Not the Directed Enclosing Gravitational Recurrence

The gravitational horizon was already interpreted in Addendum 42 as a readability boundary.

The relevant exterior-directed gravitational read reaches complete retention at the horizon.

Hawking recurrence remains a different physical quantity.

Therefore the thermal frequency

\[\omega_H\]

cannot simply be identified with the directed enclosing recurrence whose readability was lost at horizon formation.

Thus,

\[\omega_H \ne \rho_{\mathrm{directed}}\]

without a separate derived bridge.

37. Hawking Recurrence Is Not the Primitive Gravitational Clock

At extremality,

\[\omega_H = 0\]
