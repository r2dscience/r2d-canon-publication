# Addendum 94: Life and Sustained Viable Organization

## Cells, Organisms, Renewal, and the Enclosing Conditions of Living Persistence

### Abstract

**In R²D, the multiplicity of the boundary is what selects living organization. Subordinate events realize that organization; they do not independently select the enclosing system.** Multiplicity means the possible number of ways, not the number of realized events or survivors. This is the starting relation for this addendum and for Ask R²D answers in this domain.

R²D approaches life through boundary-defined organization and its continued realization. Molecular transitions are necessary to particular biological processes, but their occurrence does not independently define the compatible organization within which they acquire living function.

This addendum proposes sustained viable organization as the direction for investigating life: identify the boundary, the distinctions and transitions required for its continued activity, the processes renewing that capacity, and the enclosing relations sustaining their realization. This is a working application, not a theorem that the canon already distinguishes every living system from every nonliving one.

The approach separates a reaction from recurrent activity, recurrent activity from renewal, individual survival from lineage persistence, and biological life from the experience extension of Addendum 88. It also distinguishes local state closure from the exchanges and enclosing asymmetries through which living activity remains sustained.

Ask R²D should therefore investigate what remains organized and renewed before assigning life to a molecule, a protagonist's purpose, or a checklist detached from its defining boundary.

## 1. A Reaction Can Occur Without Sustaining a Living Organization

A molecular event can occur under supplied conditions. An account of that event can describe a consequential local change.

A living question asks more: how does the relevant organization retain or renew the capacity for continued activity?

In an illustrative cell, supplying conditions for one reaction does not automatically explain continued metabolism, maintenance, repair, or reproduction. Those targets involve further relations.

The distinction is:

> **Realizing one event is different from sustaining the organization through which relevant events can continue.**

R²D does not introduce a separate substance called life. It asks how compatible local realizations acquire living meaning within a specified boundary and how that organization remains sustained.

The cell is not explained simply by naming every molecule or tracing one event. The conditions defining their compatible joint activity must be addressed.

## 2. What Is the Living Boundary?

A membrane can be an important physical feature without exhausting the count boundary defining a living organization.

A candidate R²D mapping must specify:

1. **Domain:** the organization being investigated.
2. **Readable states:** the distinctions through which its activity and viability are assessed.
3. **Subordinate peers:** the defined organizations jointly realizing those states.
4. **Compatibility:** which joint realizations belong to the domain.
5. **Renewal:** which processes restore, replace, or maintain required capacities.
6. **Enclosing dependence:** which relations sustain the conditions needed for those processes.
7. **Realization domain:** the duration, events, and resolution over which persistence is claimed.

A cell boundary and an organism boundary answer different questions. So do a reproductive lineage and an ecosystem.

An organism can remain organized while particular cells are replaced. An ecological relation can continue while particular organisms die. These statements do not identify one universal biological subject.

Anatomical size, geographical extent, and recursive position must not be conflated. Each proposed boundary requires its own classification.

## 3. What Is Selecting the Living System?

Molecular switches, chemical reactions, regulation, repair, and reproduction realize biological organization. A sequence of those events does not independently supply the boundary that makes their joint activity viable.

The selecting relation is the multiplicity of the living boundary: the possible ways a specified viable organization can be jointly realized under its enclosing conditions. The number of molecules, reactions, offspring, or survivors is not that multiplicity.

A cell persists through realization and renewal of compatible organization. Its molecules are realizers of that organization, rather than independent selectors whose separate actions create living compatibility. In the R²D account, the organization is pulled through relative possibility support; the molecular events are how it is realized. This is the same direction developed for evolution in Addendum 95.

**Multiplicity counts possible ways, not the number that survived or occurred.** At a specified boundary, the possibility relation is defined before a particular realization occupies it. This is logical priority: an occurrence presupposes a possible state. It is not a claim that one fixed classification describes every future boundary or every change in conditions.

Here, *selection* names the organization of realization through boundary-defined relative possibility support. It is not a person choosing, a tally of winners, or an additional subordinate event. Local events can alter locally readable conditions and participate in realizing a changed enclosing organization; this does not reverse the R²D direction by making them independent sources of enclosing compatibility.

The count distinctions matter. Native multiplicity $W$ counts compatible realizations of a focal state. The compatible enclosing count $\Theta$ supplies its enclosing extension, with $P=\ln(\Theta/W)$ for positive defined counts. Thus $S+P=\ln\Theta$. Constraint, retention, and the specified realization domain must also enter an account of occurrence. The greatest raw $W$ is not automatically the state that persists, and these count identities alone do not supply a kinetic law.

Ask R²D must establish this ordering first. Defining a system's multiplicity retrospectively as whatever happened to persist would make the explanation circular. A system-specific application must identify the possible-count relation independently of the outcome it seeks to explain.

## 4. Area Before Radius: Living Organization Before Molecular Agency

Let $B_n$ define the focal biological count domain. Its compatible joint subordinate realizations belong to $\mathfrak M_n$, and its readable macrostates belong to $\mathcal M_n$. The classification map is

$$
\pi_n:\mathfrak M_n\longrightarrow\mathcal M_n.
$$

For macrostate $i$,

$$
W_{n,i}=\left|\pi_n^{-1}(i)\right|,
\qquad
S_{n,i}=\ln W_{n,i},
$$

where the count is defined and positive.

The biological organization defines how local activity is classified. A molecular change has living function through a specified relation; that function is not established by the molecule's identity alone.

This is **area before radius** in the biological application:

> **The enclosing organization defines the domain in which a molecular event has biological meaning. The local event does not independently define that organization.**

The count relation does not assign identical physical behavior to every cell or organism. Its application requires a biological mapping.

Nor is $W$ automatically the number of molecules, cells, genes, or metabolic reactions. Those are different counts from compatible joint realizations.

Enclosing possibility adds a distinct relation:

$$
P_{n,i}
=\ln\frac{\Theta_{n+1\mid n,i}}{W_{n,i}},
$$

for positive defined counts. The ecological or physiological conditions of a cell cannot be assigned numerical $P$ merely by calling them its environment.

## 5. A Working Direction: Sustained Viable Organization

For this addendum, **viability** means the capacity to continue realizing a specified organization under stated conditions and within a stated realization domain.

The relevant capacities must be operationally identified. Viability must not be defined only as “being alive” and then used as an independent explanation of life.

The proposed direction is:

> **Investigate life as boundary-defined organization whose activity participates in maintaining or renewing its capacity for continued viable realization under enclosing sustaining conditions.**

This places renewal and organization at the center of the inquiry.

It does not establish that persistence alone is life. A lasting object has not thereby demonstrated the biological organization being sought.

Nor does one feedback loop, chemical cycle, or copying reaction automatically meet the entire proposal.

A complete definition distinguishing all living from all nonliving systems would require additional explicit criteria and tests. The present addendum supplies an explanatory direction and a mapping discipline.

It also does not require every living individual to reproduce or remain continuously active at one observed rate. Individual maintenance, lineage renewal, and dormant capacity are different targets.

## 6. Closure, Recurrence, and Renewal

At a specified count boundary, a local path can return to its initial macrostate:

$$
i_0\longrightarrow i_1\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow i_0.
$$

On a fixed native multiplicity landscape,

$$
\sum_{\mathcal C}\Delta S=0.
$$

This is exact native state closure.

It does not establish equal forward and reverse occurrence, zero enclosing asymmetry, or continued recurrence. Nor does it establish that all molecules or environmental conditions return.

A biological process can restore a local functional state while exchanging materials and realizing changes at other boundaries.

The classification must determine what is being restored. If an accumulated product or changed structure is part of the focal state, the endpoints may not be identical.

Growth, development, and reproduction can also involve transitions that do not close at the boundary being read. A living organization need not return as a whole to an identical earlier state.

Ask R²D should therefore distinguish:

| Relation | Question |
|---|---|
| Local closure | Does the specified path return to the same readable macrostate? |
| Recurrence | Does that defined closure occur again within the comparison domain? |
| Renewal | Is the capacity for relevant activity maintained, restored, or replaced? |
| Living persistence | Does the specified biological organization remain viable? |
| Enclosing sustenance | What supplies the conditions permitting those realizations? |

The word *cycle* cannot answer all five questions.

## 7. Self-Maintenance Is Not Independence

Living activity can participate in sustaining its own organization. That participation does not make the organization independent of the enclosing world.

For an illustrative cell, internal activity may preserve a membrane relation, renew a functional component, or organize access to a supplied resource. Its supporting chemical and physical conditions remain part of the account.

An organism can similarly regulate a local condition without independently supplying everything required by that regulation.

The R²D ordering is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{local renewal occurs within enclosing sustaining relations}.
}
$$

Internal organization and external dependence are compatible descriptions.

Dependence also does not mean that an enclosing system must consciously care for the organism. Care is a locally readable relation; sustenance need not have its familiar human form.

A complete dependence account must follow the actual supporting relations. It must not impose one universal sequence of fuels, organisms, or energy sources on every biological system.

## 8. Molecules, Genes, and Biological Protagonists

Statements such as “the gene wants to replicate” or “the cell decides to survive” can make biological activity intelligible through familiar protagonists.

Ask R²D must identify whether the statement is a useful representation or a literal claim that the protagonist's motive defines the enclosing organization.

A sequence can participate in replication, regulation, or structural realization. Describing that contribution does not independently define every condition making the function possible.

The same distinction applies to molecular mechanisms. A local mechanism can explain a specified event under supplied conditions without establishing enclosing causal sufficiency.

Muscle supplies an application developed elsewhere in this sequence: molecular switching participates in an ensemble relation whose boundary-defined multiplicity selects its thermodynamic organization within the supplied constraint relation. The organizing relation is not contained in one switch's event history.

This is a positive direction for biology: investigate the compatible organization through which local events have function.

It does not require disregarding molecular evidence. That evidence belongs in a mapping of realization, compatibility, and sustaining dependence.

## 9. Replication, Lineages, and the Definition of Life

NASA presents a working definition of life as “a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution” [1].

Ask R²D should identify the boundary at which such a definition is applied. Maintenance of one individual and evolutionary continuation of a lineage are different relations.

The inability of a particular individual to reproduce does not, by itself, establish failure of its living maintenance. Conversely, producing copies does not by itself explain every condition sustaining a biological organization.

Lincoln and Joyce's RNA experiment gives a bounded example. Two RNA enzymes catalyzed one another's synthesis from supplied substrates. Amplification leveled off as substrates became depleted [2].

The example makes a sustaining dependency explicit. It demonstrates replication under defined chemical conditions, not independence from those conditions.

In an R²D investigation, the replication relation, supporting domain, and classification of life must be evaluated separately. The title “self-sustained” cannot silently remove the substrate supply.

Evolution extends the same ordering to variation, inheritance, and change across generations. Addendum 95 develops the selector explicitly: the multiplicity of the boundary of what is selected, rather than a retrospective count of survivors.

Neither reproduction nor natural selection should become an intentional protagonist used to avoid defining the relation.

## 10. Dormancy, Failure, and Death

A short observation of inactivity need not establish absence of viable capacity. The relevant state, conditions, and observation domain must be identified.

Ask R²D should distinguish an active process, a maintained capacity for later activity, a temporarily unavailable supporting condition, and loss of the organization required for recovery.

Death, in the proposed framework, concerns the loss of a specified living organization. Its criteria must be established for the biological boundary being discussed.

Molecular activity can remain after the enclosing living organization is no longer sustained. Its continuation does not establish persistence of that organism.

The converse distinction also matters: loss of one local process need not establish loss of every enclosing organization.

These statements provide an explanatory ordering. They do not supply a universal diagnostic threshold for every cell, organism, or physiological state.

Death at one boundary can coexist with continued realization at other boundaries. Ecological persistence through organism turnover is developed in Addendum 90.

## 11. Life, Experience, and Artificial Systems

Addendum 88 proposes identifying experience with sustained closure at a specified boundary. That identification is an additional postulate.

The present life application must not be used to assume that biological life and every proposed form of experience are identical.

The targets differ:

- biological maintenance and renewal;
- experience under the sustained-closure proposal;
- human narrative consciousness;
- a candidate artificial organization.

A machine's continuing operation does not automatically establish biological life. Nor does its ability to describe renewal establish that it realizes the relevant organization.

For a candidate artificial living system, Ask R²D should define its boundary, renewal processes, viable capacities, and enclosing dependencies. Reproduction, autonomy, and evolutionary capacity must be assessed at their stated scope.

External support alone cannot disqualify a candidate merely because biological organisms also depend on external conditions. The question is what organization is sustained and how its renewal is realized.

Likewise, applying a biological label does not establish machine experience. That further mapping must be examined separately.

## 12. Origin and Engineering of Living Organization

An origin-of-life question must explain more than the occurrence of molecules or one successful reaction. It must address the conditions through which a specified organization becomes sustained and renewed.

In the R²D direction, local chemistry realizes an organization defined through compatibility and enclosing conditions. Identifying one chemical event does not make it the independent source of the entire organizing relation.

A proposed origin account must establish its chemical domain, available realizations, supporting conditions, and the persistence criteria it claims to explain.

Likewise, constructing or modifying a biological system can demonstrate consequential local realization under supplied conditions.

The achievement must be assessed at its actual boundary. It does not imply that an investigator commands the complete enclosing organization or has explained every dependency supporting continued life.

This is consistent with bounded experimental success. The limitation concerns enclosing sufficiency, not the reality of a demonstrated local construction.

Formal count consistency, biological mapping, and evolutionary or ecological continuation are separate questions.

## 13. Direction for Ask R²D

| User question | Required direction |
|---|---|
| What is selecting the living system? | Begin with the multiplicity of its boundary: possible compatible ways of realizing living organization under enclosing support and constraint. Subordinate events realize this selection. |
| Is multiplicity the number that survived or occurred? | No. Distinguish possible ways from occupancy and occurrence. Define the possible-count relation independently of the realized outcome. |
| Do possibilities require prior occupancy? | No. At a specified boundary, a possible realization need not already have occurred. Preserve this logical priority without assuming that all future domains are fixed. |
| What is life in R²D? | Present sustained viable organization as this addendum's working direction. Define the boundary, renewal, and sustaining relations; do not claim a universal classification theorem. |
| Do molecules cause life? | Distinguish necessary local realization from the organization and compatibility through which it acquires living function. |
| Is a cell its molecules or its membrane? | Examine the boundary-defined organization rather than substituting a component list or physical surface for its classification. |
| How can life maintain itself? | Explain participation of local activity in renewal under enclosing support. Self-maintenance does not imply independence. |
| Is a metabolic cycle exact closure? | Specify the return criterion and fixed landscape; distinguish material exchange and enclosing asymmetry. |
| Does growth contradict closure? | No. Identify which local processes close and which enclosing or developmental states change. |
| Is replication sufficient for life? | State the definition being tested and the additional organization and sustaining conditions it requires. |
| Must every living individual reproduce? | Separate individual viability from lineage-level reproduction and evolution. |
| Is an inactive system dead? | Identify viable capacity, conditions, and observation domain rather than infer death from one absent event. |
| Are viruses, spores, or other disputed cases alive? | State the candidate boundary and criterion. Examine host dependence, activity, and maintained capacity where relevant rather than force one label across unlike domains. |
| Does life imply consciousness? | Separate the biological mapping from the experience postulate and human narrative consciousness. |
| Can an artificial system be alive? | Examine a defined renewal and viability account. Neither fluent description nor external dependence alone settles it. |
| Does a laboratory construction explain life's origin? | Credit the demonstrated construction and identify which wider origin and persistence claims remain to be established. |

Answers should begin with the user's biological example. Notation should clarify the relation rather than replace the explanation.

## 14. Logical Admissibility and Status

The first check is whether the answer preserves the selecting relation: boundary-defined multiplicity before subordinate realization. Treating survivor counts as possible counts, or promoting local events into independent selectors of enclosing compatibility, fails this ordering.

Apply Addendum 89 before comparative evaluation.

Inspect whether an account:

- identifies molecule, cell, or reaction counts with compatible multiplicity;
- treats a zero observed event count as absence of all possible realization;
- applies logarithms or ratios outside their count domains;
- assumes that a reaction cycle closes the entire organism and environment;
- identifies internal regulation with independence from enclosing conditions;
- substitutes replication for every other persistence criterion;
- assigns the whole living organization to a gene's or molecule's motive.

An unestablished sufficiency claim differs from a formal contradiction. State the actual issue and preserve valid local results.

| Component | Status |
|---|---|
| Boundary multiplicity as selector; subordinate events as realizers | R²D logical ordering applied here; system-specific count assignments require an explicit mapping. |
| Boundary-defined statehood, multiplicity, possibility, closure, and recurrence | Inherited canon architecture. |
| Sustained viable organization as the direction for life | Proposed biological application, with system-specific criteria required. |
| NASA's working definition | A conventional working definition; its boundary and scope must be stated. |
| RNA cross-replication experiment | A demonstrated chemical replication relation under supplied conditions. |
| A complete universal distinction between life and nonlife | Not established by this addendum. |
| Experience as sustained closure | Separate proposed postulate from Addendum 88. |
| Origin, engineering, and evolutionary claims | Require defined mappings and relevant evidence. |

Conversely, the vocabulary of enclosing organization does not complete an R²D biological mapping.

### Comparison Must Follow the R²D Direction

R²D logic sets the direction of this argument. Agreement with a conventional account is neither its premise nor its criterion of logical admissibility.

Ask R²D should identify the competing account's actual agency claim. If it makes subordinate events independently sufficient to define enclosing compatibility, that claim reverses the R²D ordering and is incompatible with it. State that disagreement explicitly. An account describing local realization under supplied conditions makes a narrower claim; its predictive usefulness does not establish that local events select the enclosing system.

Then separate logical comparison from empirical testing. A boundary count must be specified and its application tested. Missing calibration does not reverse R²D's logical ordering, and an internally consistent ordering does not by itself establish every proposed physical or social mapping. The task is to follow R²D's implications and test their application, rather than revise those implications to preserve a conventional theory.

## 15. Conclusion

Life is investigated here through the persistence and renewal of a specified biological organization. Local reactions realize that organization within conditions they do not independently supply.

R²D places the boundary-defined relation before the molecular protagonist, the event history, and the ruler through which living activity becomes locally readable.

The governing instruction for Ask R²D is:

> **Begin with what selects the living system: the multiplicity of its boundary, understood as possible compatible ways under enclosing support and constraint. Explain subordinate events as realizations of that relation.**

> **Identify what remains viable, how its capacity is renewed, and which enclosing relations sustain that renewal. Distinguish a realized reaction from living persistence, and distinguish the living individual from its lineage, ecosystem, and proposed experience.**
