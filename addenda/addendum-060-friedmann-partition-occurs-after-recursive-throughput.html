Matter-Energy, Curvature, the Cosmological Constant, and the Nonprimitive Decomposition of H 2

Addendum 60 — Friedmann Partition Occurs After Recursive Throughput

Abstract

Addendum 59 established a conditional bridge from primitive recursive counting to the physical cosmological rate.

For a dominant cosmological transition class χ, realized through a representative subordinate peer closure a,

\[ H=f_a\delta_{A,\chi}\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}.\]

where

\[ f_a\]

is the physical clock calibration,

\[\delta_{A,\chi}=\frac12\ln\frac{A_{\zeta,k}}{A_{\zeta,i}}\]

is the logarithmic geometric step associated with one cosmological occurrence, and

\[\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}=\frac{N_\chi^\zeta}{N_C^{(a)}}\]

is the recursive throughput of enclosing cosmological occurrences per representative subordinate closure.

The R²D law gives

\[\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}=\frac{\Delta A_{\zeta-1,a,C}}{C_\zeta\lambda_\zeta}\]

Thus

\[ H^2=f_a^2\delta_{A,\chi}^2\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}^2\]

This physical rate magnitude exists conceptually before the conventional Friedmann decomposition is assigned.

Define

\[ T_\zeta\equiv f_a^2\delta_{A,\chi}^2\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}^2\]

The symbol

\[ T_\zeta\]

is introduced only as shorthand for the physically calibrated recursive throughput magnitude.

It is not a new primitive R²D quantity.

Under the conditional bridge of Addendum 59,

\[ T_\zeta=H^2\]

Conventional FLRW cosmology then represents this one rate magnitude as

\[ H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}\epsilon-\frac{kc^2}{a^2}+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}\]

Define

\[ F_\epsilon=\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}\epsilon\]

\[ F_k=-\frac{kc^2}{a^2}\]

and

\[ F_\Lambda=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}\]

Then

\[ T_\zeta=H^2=F_\epsilon+F_k+F_\Lambda\]

The three terms do not have equivalent physical or logical ownership.

The matter-energy contribution

\[ F\epsilon\]

is a source-property read.

The curvature contribution

\[ Fk\]

is a geometric read.

The cosmological-constant contribution

\[ F\Lambda\]

may be retained as a geometric constant or algebraically reassigned to an effective source density.

Indeed, define

\[\epsilon_\Lambda=\frac{\Lambda c^4}{8\pi G}\]

Then

\[\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}\epsilon_\Lambda=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}\]

and the Friedmann equation becomes

\[ H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}(\epsilon+\epsilon_\Lambda)-\frac{kc^2}{a^2}\]

Likewise define an effective curvature density

\[\epsilon_k=-\frac{3kc^4}{8\pi G a^2}\]

Then

\[\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}\epsilon_k=-\frac{kc^2}{a^2}\]

and therefore

\[ H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}(\epsilon+\epsilon_\Lambda+\epsilon_k)\]

Nothing about the physical value of H 2 has changed.

Only the bookkeeping partition has changed.

The same cosmological rate magnitude can therefore be represented as

source + geometry + constant geometry,

or as

sum of effective source densities.

The decomposition itself consequently cannot determine primitive ontology.

This has an immediate R²D implication.

One must not infer identities such as

\[ P_\zeta=\epsilon_\Lambda\]

\[ R_\zeta=\epsilon_m\]

or

\[ A_\zeta=\epsilon_\Lambda-\epsilon_m\]

merely because conventional cosmological terms have suggestive signs.

The R²D quantities

\[ P_\zeta,\quad R_\zeta,\quad A_\zeta\]

belong to the pre-gravitational causal architecture.

The Friedmann terms belong to a later gravitational physical projection.

Their decomposition is representation dependent.

Thus the correct ordering is

↓

gravitational physical projection

\[ H^2\]

↓

conventional Friedmann partition

\[ F_\epsilon+F_k+F_\Lambda\]

The central statement is therefore:

Central statement. The Friedmann equation does not expose three primitive causes of cosmological evolution. It partitions one already-defined physical rate magnitude into matter-energy, curvature, and cosmological-constant coordinates within the gravitational projection. Because that partition can be rearranged without changing \(H^2\), its individual terms cannot be identified with primitive R²D quantities without independent projection bridges.

And hence:

Friedmann partition occurs after recursive throughput.

1. Addendum 59 Produced One Physical Rate Magnitude

The R²D throughput bridge gives

\[ H=f_a\delta_{A,\chi}\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}.\]

Therefore,

\[ H^2=f_a^2\delta_{A,\chi}^2\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}^2\]

This is one physical quantity.

No Friedmann partition has yet been assigned.

\[ T_\zeta\equiv f_a^2\delta_{A,\chi}^2\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}^2\]

Then

\[ T_\zeta=H^2\]

It does not add a new primitive to R²D.

\[\rho_{\zeta\mid\zeta-1,a}=\frac{\Delta A_{\zeta-1,a,C}}{C_\zeta\lambda_\zeta}\]

Therefore,

\[ T_\zeta=f_a^2\delta_{A,\chi}^2\left(\frac{\Delta A_{\zeta-1,a,C}}{C_\zeta\lambda_\zeta}\right)^2\]

The dimensionless recursive relation has acquired:

\[ f_a\]

a temporal calibration,

and

\[\delta_{A,\chi}\]

a geometric calibration.

\[ T_\zeta\]

belongs to the physical projection layer.

\[ T_\zeta=H^2=F_\epsilon+F_k+F_\Lambda\]

The Friedmann equation partitions the rate magnitude among physical coordinates.

The notation makes them look like three additive terms of one type.

They are not.

They enter the physical theory from different descriptive categories.
