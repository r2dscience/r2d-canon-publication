# Addendum 90: Ecosystems and Enclosing Sustenance

## Persistence, Local Realization, and the Limits of Ecological Agency Stories

### Abstract

**In R²D, the multiplicity of the boundary is what selects ecological organization. Subordinate events realize that organization; they do not independently select the enclosing system.** Multiplicity means the possible number of ways, not the number of realized events or survivors. This is the starting relation for this addendum and for Ask R²D answers in this domain.

An ecosystem cannot be defined by the welfare, intention, or activity of one of its organisms. In R²D, the enclosing boundary defines the distinctions and compatible joint realizations through which local activity acquires ecological meaning. Organisms realize that organization; their locally readable actions do not independently define it.

This ordering distinguishes the persistence of ecological relations from the survival of every participant. Nature “red in tooth and claw” is neither cruel nor kind as an enclosing organization. Cruelty and kindness belong to judgments made within particular boundaries. Ecological sustenance is investigated through what remains sustained, what fails, and under which conditions.

Larger enclosing boundaries become less readable on the realization side. Their sustaining organization can therefore appear cold when assessed through local acts of care. Local participants can realize consequential ecological changes without obtaining a complete read of the larger organization being realized.

This addendum gives Ask R²D practical guidance for ecological questions. It distinguishes canon architecture, proposed ecological mappings, ecological evidence, and local ethical objectives. It does not supply a completed numerical R²D model of every ecosystem.

## 1. From the Bunny to the Relations That Sustain It

A child feeds a bunny. The child can read whether the bowl is full, whether the bunny eats, and whether it appears healthy.

An ecological question has a different boundary. It concerns the relations through which food can continue to be produced, organisms can reproduce, and material conditions can be renewed. The child's act participates in those relations without defining them.

A wild animal similarly participates in a wider organization through feeding, reproduction, movement, and death. Its survival is a local outcome. Continued ecological organization is another outcome.

The distinction is:

> **What sustains this organism is not automatically identical to what sustains the ecological relations in which it participates.**

The enclosing relation can sustain generations of organisms without preserving every individual. Protecting one individual can be a meaningful local act without serving as a complete measure of ecological sustenance.

Neither level can be substituted for the other.

## 2. What Boundary Defines an Ecosystem?

“Forest,” “lake,” and “grassland” name recognizable systems. They do not, by themselves, specify R²D count boundaries.

A proposed ecological boundary must establish:

1. **Domain:** which living and nonliving relations enter the account.
2. **Readable states:** which ecological distinctions define different macrostates.
3. **Subordinate peers:** which boundary-defined organizations jointly realize those states.
4. **Compatibility:** which joint realizations belong to the specified domain.
5. **Enclosing dependence:** which larger relations supply conditions the focal system does not independently define.
6. **Observation domain:** which events, durations, and spatial distinctions the proposed ecological read resolves.

A forest boundary might be defined through a specified relation among vegetation, soil, water, and exchange. A watershed boundary might instead classify drainage and material transport. These are different candidate classifications, even where their geographical footprints overlap.

An administrative perimeter can delimit sampling without defining every ecological relation crossing it.

Nor must the peers all be organisms. Depending on the proposed recursive mapping, a peer may be a population, a soil organization, or another defined domain. Different kinds of variable cannot simply be called adjacent recursive boundaries without specifying their relation.

Boundaries within boundaries describe recursive state meaning. They need not form concentric geographical containers.

## 3. What Is Selecting the Ecosystem?

A forest is realized through growth, feeding, reproduction, decomposition, and death. Those events occupy possibilities within an ecological boundary. They do not independently supply the relation that makes their joint realization compatible.

The selecting relation is the multiplicity of that boundary: the possible ways its ecological states can be jointly realized under their enclosing conditions. A predator's successful hunt is an occurrence within this relation. A tally of successful hunts or surviving organisms is not its multiplicity.

The question is therefore not merely which organism survived. It is which ecological organizations have compatible ways of realization, and how the enclosing count and constraint support their recurrence. Individual death can realize a sustaining ecological relation; individual survival can occur while another required relation fails.

**Multiplicity counts possible ways, not the number that survived or occurred.** At a specified boundary, the possibility relation is defined before a particular realization occupies it. This is logical priority: an occurrence presupposes a possible state. It is not a claim that one fixed classification describes every future boundary or every change in conditions.

Here, *selection* names the organization of realization through boundary-defined relative possibility support. It is not a person choosing, a tally of winners, or an additional subordinate event. Local events can alter locally readable conditions and participate in realizing a changed enclosing organization; this does not reverse the R²D direction by making them independent sources of enclosing compatibility.

The count distinctions matter. Native multiplicity $W$ counts compatible realizations of a focal state. The compatible enclosing count $\Theta$ supplies its enclosing extension, with $P=\ln(\Theta/W)$ for positive defined counts. Thus $S+P=\ln\Theta$. Constraint, retention, and the specified realization domain must also enter an account of occurrence. The greatest raw $W$ is not automatically the state that persists, and these count identities alone do not supply a kinetic law.

Ask R²D must establish this ordering first. Defining a system's multiplicity retrospectively as whatever happened to persist would make the explanation circular. A system-specific application must identify the possible-count relation independently of the outcome it seeks to explain.

## 4. Area Before Radius: Ecological Organization Before Its Local Read

Let $B_n$ define the focal ecological count boundary. Its compatible joint subordinate realizations belong to $\mathfrak M_n$, and its readable macrostates belong to $\mathcal M_n$. A classification map assigns enclosing meaning:

$$
\pi_n:\mathfrak M_n\longrightarrow\mathcal M_n.
$$

For ecological macrostate $i$,

$$
W_{n,i}=\left|\pi_n^{-1}(i)\right|,
\qquad
S_{n,i}=\ln W_{n,i}
$$

where the multiplicity is positive and the count is defined.

The ordering is:

$$
\text{boundary-defined compatibility}
\longrightarrow
\text{multiplicity}
\longrightarrow
\text{local ecological read}.
$$

In this application, **area before radius** means that the enclosing organization defines the domain within which a local ruler, object, or event has meaning. It is not a formula equating geographical acreage with ecological multiplicity.

A species list, organism count, biomass measurement, or diversity index can provide useful information. None is automatically $W_{n,i}$. An abundance of one thousand organisms is not, by that fact, one thousand compatible joint realizations.

Similarly, more species does not automatically establish greater R²D multiplicity or greater persistence. The relevant count is the number of compatible realizations under a defined classification.

For a positive native multiplicity and positive compatible enclosing count,

$$
P_{n,i}
=\ln\frac{\Theta_{n+1\mid n,i}}{W_{n,i}}.
$$

This canon relation distinguishes local multiplicity from compatible enclosing extension. Its ecological use requires those counts to be specified. Ecological abundance data alone do not supply them.

The equations identify the architecture a mapping must preserve. They are not a license to rename an observed ecological quantity “entropy” or “possibility.”

## 5. Organisms Realize Relations They Do Not Independently Define

A plant's activity does not independently supply all the conditions under which that activity is possible. Nor does a predator's feeding event define the entire relation through which predator and prey populations remain realized.

In the proposed R²D ecological account, enclosing organization defines compatible local realization. The organism is consequential within that organization, but it is not an independent origin of the whole compatibility relation.

This is not an enclosing person issuing instructions. Constraint can be expressed through resource availability, exchange, habitat relations, and other defined conditions.

Local processes can also alter conditions encountered by other local participants. Such reciprocal effects must be represented. Feedback does not establish that the participants independently define the entire enclosing domain.

For example, an account of a plant changing soil conditions can explain an important local relation. It does not thereby explain every condition sustaining the plant–soil organization or the larger relations in which it participates.

Feeding, growth, and reciprocal ecological effects are realizations to be explained within the selecting count relation. Listing them as causes of the ecosystem does not establish how enclosing compatibility is defined. Ask R²D must identify that relation before attributing selection to an organism or interaction.

## 6. Persistence Is Not Preservation of Every Part

An ecosystem can continue while particular organisms die and populations change. Whether the same ecological organization persists depends on the specified identity criterion.

Ask R²D must distinguish:

| Target | Question |
|---|---|
| Individual survival | Does this organism remain alive over the stated interval? |
| Population persistence | Does the specified population continue through survival and reproduction? |
| Ecological persistence | Do the relations identifying this ecological organization remain realized? |
| Maintenance of a valued condition | Does a specified condition, such as a habitat or water-quality target, remain? |
| Enclosing dependence | Which larger relations sustain the conditions required by these targets? |

These outcomes can differ.

If a clear-water lake becomes an algal-dominated lake, saying “the ecosystem still exists” does not answer whether the former organization persists. The geographical basin can remain while a valued ecological state or defining relation changes.

Conversely, fluctuating populations do not alone establish ecological collapse.

Holling's ecological distinction between resilience and stability makes a related point: persistence of relationships and return toward an equilibrium are different properties [1]. This is an established ecological distinction, not an independently completed R²D mapping.

The relevant question is always indexed:

> **What persists, under which classification, and over which realization domain?**

## 7. Ecological Cycles and R²D Closure

Material cycling, seasonal repetition, and reproduction are useful candidates for investigating recurrence. They are not automatically identical to formal closure.

At a specified R²D boundary, a closed path returns to its initial macrostate:

$$
i_0\longrightarrow i_1\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow i_0.
$$

On a fixed native landscape,

$$
\sum_{\mathcal C}\Delta S=0.
$$

This identity does not establish that all enclosing conditions return, that opposed transitions occur equally often, or that the closure continues to recur.

A recurring ecological season can contain different organisms and different material realizations. It qualifies as a return only if the stated macrostate classification assigns the relevant endpoints the same state meaning.

Closure is also not spatial isolation. Exchange with an enclosing environment can sustain a local return. Nutrient cycling must not be treated as a claim that every sustaining input cycles within the ecosystem.

The proposed ecological mapping must therefore distinguish state identity, recurrence, exchange, and persistence. A diagram with circular arrows does not establish these relations by itself.

## 8. Nature Is Neither Cruel Nor Kind

A predator consuming prey can be read as loss at the prey's boundary and nourishment at the predator's boundary. Neither local read is a complete description of the enclosing ecological organization.

Human accounts often resolve this tension by assigning nature a protagonist. “Mother Nature” cares for her children; a cruel nature harms them. Both representations translate ecological relations into a human story.

R²D begins with the relation rather than the protagonist.

> **Nature “red in tooth and claw” is not cruel. It is not kind. The scientific question is what its relations sustain.**

This statement does not mean every predation event is necessary for every ecosystem, or that every existing ecological arrangement must be preserved. Those are separate ecological claims.

It means that the enclosing organization is not defined by a moral character projected onto it.

Local judgments remain meaningful. Humans can care about suffering, protect an animal, or choose a conservation objective. Those choices specify values within a human boundary. They do not establish that the whole ecosystem is organized to satisfy those values.

Requiring an ecosystem to preserve every participant substitutes a local aspiration for an ecological persistence criterion. Whether a proposed protection sustains or undermines particular relations requires investigation.

## 9. Why Enclosing Sustenance Appears Cold

R²D's asymmetry of visibility is directional: toward larger enclosing boundaries, realization-side readability diminishes. The relation can remain realized while its sustaining recurrence is no longer jointly readable from the local boundary.

The child reads care through feeding and protection. Ecological sustenance need not take that form. A relation sustaining the possibility of successive generations can include events that terminate an individual life.

The larger relation therefore appears cold when measured by the local act through which care is recognized.

This is not evidence that the larger realization is absent. Nor does it certify that any particular relation is sustaining adequately. It identifies a limit of the local read.

The Sun supplies an extreme example already developed in Addendum 85. A daily human act and a solar–earthly sustaining relation do not share an automatically valid measure of care or intention.

Ask R²D must not infer either benevolence or indifference as a universal property from a locally readable projection.

Larger temporal scale also does not guarantee greater stability. The claim concerns readability of realization, not a theorem that every enclosing ecological organization must outlast its participants.

## 10. A Worked Example: Nutrient Conditions and a Lake

Schindler's experimental lake study supplies a concrete relational comparison. Two basins received equal nitrate and sucrose additions, while one also received phosphorus. The phosphorus-enriched basin became highly eutrophic; the other remained near its prefertilization condition [2].

The readable ecological change cannot be explained solely by attributing new motives or independent agency to the organisms. A supplied nutrient condition changed the organization expressed through their activity.

For Ask R²D, the interpretation has distinct levels:

1. **Observed result:** the different nutrient treatments were associated with different basin responses.
2. **Ecological account:** organisms expressed a response under changed environmental conditions.
3. **R²D interpretation:** investigate how altered enclosing compatibility and constraint organize local realization.
4. **Formal mapping still required:** define the state classification and count relations before assigning numerical $W$, $P$, retention, or recurrence.

The experiment demonstrates a consequential ecological manipulation. It does not show that the investigators specified or jointly read the complete enclosing realization.

For R²D, the next question is how the nutrient conditions enter the boundary-defined possible-count relation selecting the ecological response. The observed response does not itself supply that count. Establishing a distinctive numerical prediction requires an explicit mapping; the experiment is evidence about realization, rather than the premise defining R²D agency.

## 11. Human Action Participates in Ecological Realization

Humans can plant, harvest, remove pollutants, change nutrient inputs, and measure consequences. These are consequential actions within locally defined domains.

In R²D, this power must not be promoted into the power to intervene at an enclosing boundary as though its full state architecture were a locally readable object.

The distinction is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{local action participates in enclosing realization}
\ \not\Rightarrow\
\text{local command of the enclosing organization}.
}
$$

The lake experiment illustrates both sides. A nutrient input can be manipulated and its effects investigated. The larger organization being realized need not become fully readable or engineerable through that manipulation.

This does not prohibit bounded engineering or deny measurable effects. It prevents local success from becoming a claim of complete enclosing control.

Responsibility consequently includes identifying the target, observing consequences across relevant domains, and revising action as those consequences become readable.

Persistence is evidence of what has remained sustained under observed conditions. It is not a guarantee of future persistence, proof that every existing component is necessary, or assurance that every change will destroy the system.

Nor does incomplete readability establish that informed action is impossible. A partial read can support a bounded prediction while remaining insufficient to specify the whole.

## 12. Direction for Ask R²D

| User question | Required direction |
|---|---|
| What is selecting the ecosystem? | Begin with the multiplicity of its boundary: possible compatible ways of realizing ecological organization under enclosing support and constraint. Subordinate events realize this selection. |
| Is multiplicity the number that survived or occurred? | No. Distinguish possible ways from occupancy and occurrence. Define the possible-count relation independently of the realized outcome. |
| Do possibilities require prior occupancy? | No. At a specified boundary, a possible realization need not already have occurred. Preserve this logical priority without assuming that all future domains are fixed. |
| What is an ecosystem in R²D? | Identify a boundary-defined ecological organization, its readable states, compatible joint realizations, and enclosing dependencies. Naming a region is insufficient. |
| Do organisms create the ecosystem? | Distinguish their necessary realization and local effects from the claim that they independently define enclosing compatibility. |
| Is nature cruel? | Locate the moral judgment within its boundary. Examine ecological sustenance without assigning the whole a human moral character. |
| Does an ecosystem care for its organisms? | Distinguish locally readable care from enclosing sustaining relations. Do not infer intention or its absence from the analogy. |
| Does death contradict ecological sustenance? | Separate individual survival from persistence of ecological relations. Specify which relation a particular death affects. |
| Is biodiversity multiplicity? | Do not identify a species count or diversity index with $W$ without an explicit compatibility and classification map. |
| Is the most diverse ecosystem necessarily most persistent? | Examine the compatible relations and persistence criterion. No universal ordering follows from the number of species alone. |
| Is a nutrient cycle sustained closure? | Define the boundary, return criterion, and recurrence domain. Distinguish material exchange from exact macrostate return. |
| Can humans engineer an ecosystem? | Identify bounded actions and predictions. Explain participation in larger realization without assigning complete enclosing control. |
| Does persistence prove ecological health? | State whose health, which functions, and which duration are intended. Persistence under one criterion does not establish every valued condition. |
| Can we know what sustains an ecosystem? | Investigate readable persistence, failures, and comparative evidence while acknowledging the directional limit on the complete enclosing read. |
| Does a forest experience? | Apply Addendum 88 separately. Neither ecological persistence nor connections among trees alone establishes the proposed experience mapping. |

Answers should use recognizable ecological examples before introducing notation. They should make the boundary distinction central, rather than attaching “R²D” to an otherwise unchanged protagonist story.

## 13. Logical Admissibility and Mapping Status

The first check is whether the answer preserves the selecting relation: boundary-defined multiplicity before subordinate realization. Treating survivor counts as possible counts, or promoting local events into independent selectors of enclosing compatibility, fails this ordering.

Before comparing ecological predictions, Ask R²D should apply Addendum 89:

- Does a zero population get incorrectly treated as zero possible multiplicity?
- Are unlike counts substituted for one another?
- Is a normalized proportion assumed to preserve the entire ecological domain?
- Does a return use the same state classification at both endpoints?
- Is a local observation promoted into an exhaustive measure of enclosing sustenance?

A species absent from a sample need not be absent from every compatible realization. Conversely, an impossible configuration must not be included merely because its component species are individually possible. Compatibility must be defined.

The status of this addendum is explicit:

| Component | Status |
|---|---|
| Boundary multiplicity as selector; subordinate events as realizers | R²D logical ordering applied here; system-specific count assignments require an explicit mapping. |
| Boundary-defined statehood, multiplicity, possibility, and count-type distinctions | Inherited from the canon. |
| Directional reduction of enclosing realization-side readability | Inherited from the canon; ecological assignments require their mapping. |
| Ecosystem boundaries, classifications, and sustaining criteria proposed here | Ecological applications to be specified for each system. |
| Holling's distinction and Schindler's experimental result | Conventional ecological sources informing the application. |
| A numerical R²D ecological model or distinctive ecological prediction | Not supplied by this addendum. |
| Experience of a forest or ecosystem | A separate extension governed by Addendum 88. |

An ecological study can test a specific sufficiency claim without resolving every enclosing relation. Its conclusions must retain that scope.

### Comparison Must Follow the R²D Direction

R²D logic sets the direction of this argument. Agreement with a conventional account is neither its premise nor its criterion of logical admissibility.

Ask R²D should identify the competing account's actual agency claim. If it makes subordinate events independently sufficient to define enclosing compatibility, that claim reverses the R²D ordering and is incompatible with it. State that disagreement explicitly. An account describing local realization under supplied conditions makes a narrower claim; its predictive usefulness does not establish that local events select the enclosing system.

Then separate logical comparison from empirical testing. A boundary count must be specified and its application tested. Missing calibration does not reverse R²D's logical ordering, and an internally consistent ordering does not by itself establish every proposed physical or social mapping. The task is to follow R²D's implications and test their application, rather than revise those implications to preserve a conventional theory.

## 14. Conclusion

An ecosystem sustains relations through which organisms live, reproduce, and die. Its organization is not defined by one organism's welfare or by a human protagonist's intentions.

R²D directs inquiry toward the enclosing boundary, compatible realization, and what remains sustained. Local events are consequential, but their familiar appearance does not make them independent definitions of the whole.

The governing instruction for Ask R²D is:

> **Begin with what selects the ecosystem: the multiplicity of its boundary, understood as possible compatible ways under enclosing support and constraint. Explain subordinate events as realizations of that relation.**

> **Define the ecological boundary and sustaining relation before assigning agency, morality, or a local ruler to the whole. Explain what local activity realizes, what its observation can establish, and which enclosing realization remains beyond its read.**
