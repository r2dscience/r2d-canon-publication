# Addendum 84: Paradox as Boundary Projection

## Gibbs, Zeno, and the Coastline: Counts, Coordinates, and Rulers Beyond Their Defining Distinctions

### Abstract

Scientific paradoxes can arise when a relation defined at one count boundary is treated as invariant after the conditions defining that relation change. Recursively Realized Distinguishability (R²D) makes those conditions explicit: a boundary defines distinguishable macrostates, compatible subordinate realizations define their multiplicities, and physical coordinates express later reads of this state architecture. A change in distinguishability can therefore require a change in the count, coordinate, or ruler through which an object is represented.

Gibbs, Zeno, and the coastline problem expose three aspects of this error. Gibbs preserves a count as its defining distinction disappears. A physical reading of Zeno preserves an indefinitely divisible coordinate without establishing that every subdivision remains a readable distinction of the same object. The coastline problem preserves the expectation of one scale-independent length while changing the resolution that defines the measured boundary. In R²D, the enclosing boundary defines the subordinate ruler; the ruler is not assumed to be a boundary-independent primitive.

A binary mechanical system motivated by muscle supplies a concrete thermodynamic example. Its equilibrium entropic force contains a $1/d$ dependence, where $d$ is a mechanical distinction between states. Holding the reaction entropy fixed while taking $d\to0$ produces a formal divergence. However, the mechanical force available to drive a discrete unmixing step scales as $\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}d$, and its associated capacity scales as $\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}d^2$. The analysis gives a finite threshold at which this driving force can no longer overcome the entropic resistance. This establishes a physical limitation on the continuation of the specified reversible process, rather than an arbitrary numerical cutoff.

The common diagnostic is to identify the relation being held invariant and test whether its defining distinction survives the proposed limit or boundary change. The muscle example demonstrates this mechanism in one specified system. Its extension to particular continuum, gravitational, or cosmological singularities remains a mathematical program.

## 1. Boundary Accounting Comes Before Physical Projection

Let $B_n$ define a count domain, with readable macrostates $i\in\mathcal M_n$. Compatible joint realizations of subordinate peers belong to $\mathfrak M_n$, and a boundary-defined classification map assigns them to macrostates:

$$
\pi_n:\mathfrak M_n\longrightarrow\mathcal M_n.
$$

The multiplicity of macrostate $i$ is

$$
W_{n,i}=\left|\pi_n^{-1}(i)\right|,
$$

with the dimensionless entropy coordinate

$$
S_{n,i}=\ln W_{n,i}.
$$

Under a thermodynamic read, the corresponding dimensional entropy is $k_BS_{n,i}$. The primitive count relation and its physical representation should therefore remain distinct.

The logical ordering is

$$
B_n
\longrightarrow
\text{distinguishable states and compatible realizations}
\longrightarrow
W_{n,i}
\longrightarrow
\text{physical read}.
$$

A physical read may express entropy, force, displacement, radius, or another coordinate. Its validity depends on the state architecture being read. A formula can remain mathematically evaluable after the physical conditions defining its variables have failed.

*Area Before Radius* states the corresponding coordinate precedence as [2]

$$
B_n\longrightarrow x_{n-1},
$$

and, under a spatial read,

$$
\mathcal A_n\longrightarrow r_{n-1}.
$$

These arrows express definitional precedence, not temporal evolution. A subordinate coordinate is defined relative to an enclosing boundary. Resolving previously unreadable subordinate transitions can require another count boundary and another state classification. Greater resolution need not preserve the same object under a finer description.

We call a paradox generated by an unsupported preservation of such a relation a **boundary-projection paradox**. The diagnostic does not presume that every apparent contradiction has this origin. It asks whether the conditions required to define the disputed quantity have been preserved.

## 2. Gibbs: Count Beyond Distinguishability

Consider a binary ensemble with populations $N_B$ and $N_Y$, where $N=N_B+N_Y$. When the two alternatives are distinguishable under the specified classification,

$$
W=\frac{N!}{N_B!N_Y!},
\qquad
S_{\mathrm{th}}=k_B\ln W.
$$

For a transition $B\to Y$,

$$
\frac{W(N_B-1,N_Y+1)}{W(N_B,N_Y)}
=\frac{N_B}{N_Y+1},
$$

so

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}}
=k_B\ln\frac{N_B}{N_Y+1}.
$$

The distinction between $B$ and $Y$ participates in defining this count. If that distinction disappears at the counting boundary, exchanging the former labels no longer defines additional distinguishable realizations. The multiplicity associated with that binary distinction must be redefined. Other physical degrees of freedom can remain; losing one distinction does not imply that the entire system has only one microstate.

The erroneous continuation is

$$
d\to0
\quad\text{with the original binary classification held fixed}.
$$

The 2023 mechanical analysis develops this issue through a two-state reaction [1]. *Area Before Radius* gives its R²D formulation: the count relation changes when the distinction defining it ceases to be readable [2].

The apparent disappearance of a mixing contribution is therefore a change in what is counted. Continuity of a physical parameter alone does not establish invariance of the classification through which entropy is defined.

## 3. Zeno: Coordinate Beyond Distinguishability

The series

$$
L=\frac{L}{2}+\frac{L}{4}+\frac{L}{8}+\cdots
$$

converges. Its convergence resolves the mathematical sum; R²D adds a question about its physical interpretation.

Does every interval $L/2^k$ remain a readable distinction of the same boundary-defined object under the same physical coordinate?

An ideal mathematical coordinate permits unlimited subdivision. That permission does not establish unlimited physical readability. The inference requiring examination is

$$
\text{mathematical subdivision}
\Longrightarrow
\text{additional physical states of the same object}.
$$

In R²D, finer resolution can require moving to the boundary at which a subordinate distinction becomes readable. The resulting classification need not be the original classification continued without limit.

This is the coordinate counterpart of Gibbs:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Gibbs:}&\quad\text{retain the count as its defining distinction changes},\\
\text{Zeno:}&\quad\text{retain the physical coordinate through unlimited subdivision}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The shared issue is preservation of a derived read without establishing preservation of its defining distinction. This interpretation does not require a universal minimum length. The relevant limit is boundary-relative, and must be established for the system under consideration.

## 4. Muscle: A Finite Mechanical Test

The binary mechanical system in [1] gives this argument physical content. A transition between states $B$ and $Y$ produces displacement $d$. For the equilibrium states treated in that model,

$$
F=\frac{k_BT}{d}\ln\frac{N_B}{N_Y+1}
=\frac{T\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}}}{d}.
$$

Holding temperature and a nonzero reaction-entropy difference fixed gives

$$
|F|\propto\frac{1}{d},
$$

and formal extrapolation produces $|F|\to\infty$ as $d\to0$. The divergence depends on preserving the same entropy difference and equilibrium-state interpretation through this limit.

The discrete mechanical analysis also includes the force change produced by a single chemical step. Its magnitude is

$$
F_{\mathrm{drive}}=\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}d,
$$

where $\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}$ is the system stiffness. The entropic resistance to a specified unmixing step is

$$
F_{\mathrm{resist}}=\frac{T\Delta\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}}}{d}.
$$

Here $\Delta\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}}$ is the positive increment in reaction entropy entering that resistive-force comparison. It is a difference between successive reaction-entropy values, not the total entropy of mixing. The force magnitudes are used to avoid conflating resistance with the signed mechanical force change.

The equality of driving and resistive magnitudes gives [1, Eq. 7]

$$
\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}d
=\frac{T\Delta\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}}}{d},
$$

or

$$
\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}d^2=T\Delta\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}}.
$$

For the specified positive entropy increment and stiffness,

$$
\boxed{
d_{\mathrm{crit}}
=\sqrt{\frac{T\Delta\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}}}{\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}}}.
}
$$

Below this threshold, the finite mechanical driving force cannot sustain that unmixing step against the entropic resistance. The paper also gives the external force increment for the step as

$$
\Delta F_{\mathrm{ext}}
=\frac{T\Delta\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}}}{d}+\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}d
$$

[1, Eq. 6]. Beyond the stall point, the paper describes additional external force as uncoupled from the reversible chemistry: stronger pulling can detach molecules or damage the system without driving further reversible unmixing. The threshold therefore belongs to the specified mechanical process and its finite molecular driving capacity.

The important result is that the reversible sequence used to give the entropic-force coordinate operational meaning has a finite mechanical condition of validity. Its continuation to arbitrarily small $d$ is not licensed by the equilibrium formula alone.

## 5. The Capacity to Retain an Entropic Difference

The force comparison has an equivalent energetic form:

$$
E_{\mathrm{capacity}}=\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}d^2,
\qquad
E_{\mathrm{resist}}=T\Delta\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}}.
$$

$E_{\mathrm{capacity}}$ is the force-times-step scale used in the discrete comparison. It should not be confused with the total stored energy $\tfrac12\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}x^2$ of a Hookean spring.

The specified unmixing step requires

$$
\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}d^2>T\Delta\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}},
$$

with equality marking its stall threshold. The 2023 paper interprets this finite mechanical capacity as a physical container for the entropic difference [1]. As $d$ decreases, the capacity decreases quadratically. The accessible extent of unmixing changes with it.

This distinction matters. Stalling of a particular unmixing process does not, by itself, prove that the molecular labels immediately disappear at $d_{\mathrm{crit}}$. What it establishes is a limit on mechanically retaining or reversing the specified entropy difference. The stronger description of this limit as landscape replacement belongs to the R²D interpretation and requires identification of the changed readable states.

Under that interpretation, one cannot preserve the original operational state architecture simply by continuing its force formula. The physical capacity defining the accessible landscape must enter the count and coordinate description.

Gibbs and the apparent force singularity thus meet at the same requirement: the distinction supporting the count must be physically accounted for. The force law cannot be extrapolated independently of that support.

## 6. Coastline: The Boundary Defines Its Ruler

The coastline problem gives the geometric counterpart. Let $N(\epsilon)$ be the number of measuring segments of length $\epsilon$ required under a specified covering procedure. The measured perimeter is approximately

$$
L(\epsilon)=N(\epsilon)\epsilon.
$$

Reducing $\epsilon$ resolves additional structure and changes $N(\epsilon)$. Expecting one invariant length while changing the rule of resolution is therefore an additional assumption.

For an ideal fractal scaling model over a specified range,

$$
N(\epsilon)\sim C\epsilon^{-D},
$$

and hence

$$
L(\epsilon)\sim C\epsilon^{1-D}.
$$

For $D>1$, extending that idealization to $\epsilon\to0$ gives a divergent length. This is a valid mathematical property of the idealized curve. Applying the same scaling law to a physical coast at every resolution requires a separate physical justification.

The R²D interpretation goes beyond recognizing that the measured length depends on scale. The ruler itself belongs to a boundary-defined state architecture. As *Area Before Radius* describes it, a perimeter at one boundary is realized by a multiplicity of subordinate measures, each of which can be realized through measures at a further subordinate boundary [2, Section 13]. Changing the boundary can change both the multiplicity and the ruler.

Schematically,

$$
B_n\longrightarrow\epsilon_{n-1},
\qquad
L_n=N_n\epsilon_{n-1}.
$$

The product expresses a measurement read; it does not establish that $\epsilon_{n-1}$ is a primitive ruler independent of $B_n$. The enclosing boundary defines which subordinate distinctions count as measures of that perimeter.

Under a spatial read, this is the same precedence expressed by

$$
\mathcal A_n\longrightarrow r_{n-1}.
$$

The R²D claim is definitional: the area or boundary determines the admissible ruler through which it is represented. It is not the algebraic discovery that radius can be calculated from area.

The coastline therefore joins Gibbs and Zeno naturally. Gibbs questions the count, Zeno questions the coordinate, and coastline questions the presumption of a universal ruler. All three ask whether a derived relation can retain its physical meaning while the distinctions supporting it change.

## 7. A Conditional Singularity Statement

The muscle example supports a precise general statement. Suppose a physical read has the form

$$
Q(d)=\frac{a}{d},
$$

with a fixed, nonzero numerator $a$. Suppose further that the operational state architecture defining this relation is admissible only for

$$
d\ge d_{\mathrm{crit}}>0.
$$

Then, on that admissible domain,

$$
|Q(d)|\le\frac{|a|}{d_{\mathrm{crit}}}.
$$

The formal divergence at $d=0$ lies outside the specified domain. This elementary implication becomes physically informative when $d_{\mathrm{crit}}$ is established independently from the system's capacity, rather than chosen to suppress the divergence.

For the mechanical comparison,

$$
a=T\Delta\Delta S_{\mathrm{th}},
$$

and the threshold follows from the competing mechanical force. For other systems, both the numerator and the admissibility condition may change with state. Their dependence must be retained.

The proposed R²D mechanism can be summarized as **continuity beyond distinguishability**: a coordinate is continued after the physical state relation required to read it has failed. This describes one mechanism of apparent physical singularity. It is not a definition of every mathematical singularity.

One example is sufficient to demonstrate that a fixed state architecture cannot universally be continued through every coordinate limit. It is not sufficient to classify every singularity in Navier–Stokes theory, general relativity, or cosmology. Each application requires its own state map and independently established validity condition.

## 8. The Ultraviolet Catastrophe: A Thermal Read Beyond Joint Readability

The canon already connects the ultraviolet catastrophe, Gibbs, and the muscle thought experiment through the same boundary-projection error [3, Sections 4.15–4.18]. That connection begins with R²D recurrence, asymmetry, and admissible occupancy. Electromagnetic modes and physical frequency are not primitive definitions of these relations.

Repeatable occupancy permits a count $m=0,1,2,\ldots$. For a positive dimensionless asymmetry $a$ against additional occupancy, the relative weights form a geometric series:

$$
Z(a)=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}e^{-ma}
=\frac{1}{1-e^{-a}}.
$$

The mean occupancy is therefore

$$
\bar m(a)
=\frac{\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}m e^{-ma}}{\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}e^{-ma}}
=\frac{1}{e^a-1}.
$$

Here $a$ is a local notation for the occupancy asymmetry. This count result requires neither a primitive photon nor an electromagnetic mode. It precedes the blackbody physical mapping.

The blackbody supplies a thermal boundary within which this count structure acquires an observable read. Subordinate recurrence is compared with the enclosing background recurrence scale. The relation governing occupancy is therefore boundary-relative: changing subordinate recurrence relative to background changes its participation in the enclosing thermal count.

Only after that architecture is established does the canon introduce the empirical physical calibration

$$
a\longmapsto\frac{hf}{k_BT}.
$$

In this mapping, $f$ is physical frequency, a read of recurrence; it is not the primitive recurrence count or the R²D occurrence count. Likewise, $hf$ is an energetic read. These physical coordinates do not define the primitive count architecture.

The mapped occupancy is

$$
\bar m=\frac{1}{e^{hf/(k_BT)}-1}.
$$

Its significance for this addendum is the ordering:

$$
\text{boundary and background}
\longrightarrow
\text{relative recurrence and asymmetry}
\longrightarrow
\text{repeatable occupancy}
\longrightarrow
\text{physical spectral read}.
$$

The classical thermal expression is a limiting read of this relation. It is valid where subordinate recurrence is slow relative to the enclosing thermal scale. The ultraviolet error is to preserve that limiting expression as subordinate recurrence moves beyond the regime of joint thermodynamic readability.

The canon states the structural error directly: increasingly rapid subordinate recurrence is treated as though the same jointly readable thermodynamic relation remains valid without limit [3, Section 4.15]. The error is therefore not primitively a proliferation of electromagnetic modes. It is the unsupported continuation of a boundary-specific thermal read across recursive scale.

This is the connection to muscle. In the muscle example, an enclosing mechanical relation is extrapolated while the molecular distinction needed to sustain the accessible landscape approaches its capacity limit. In the blackbody example, the classical thermal relation is extrapolated while subordinate recurrence leaves the regime in which its associated distinguishability remains jointly readable. Both preserve a physical read after the conditions supporting that read change.

Their responses differ. The muscle analysis identifies a finite capacity for a specified reversible unmixing step. At the blackbody quantum horizon, recurrence remains consequential while its associated subordinate geometry is no longer jointly readable; occupancy changes with its relation to background. The canon explicitly distinguishes this horizon from landscape collapse [3, Section 4.17]. No universal cutoff or disappearance of recurrence is required.

Thus the ultraviolet catastrophe belongs here as an existing canon example of the same projection error:

$$
\boxed{
\text{A limiting thermal read is continued beyond its boundary-relative conditions of readability.}
}
$$

This argument uses the canon's count-to-physics ordering. Deriving every geometric factor in a complete spectral read is a separate task from identifying the failed extrapolation.

## 9. Direction, Measurement, and Information

The same diagnostic can be extended to other paradoxes, with their distinct evidentiary burdens retained.

**Loschmidt.** Reversing subordinate velocities reverses a possible mechanical realization. It does not automatically reverse the enclosing multiplicity relation or the statistical support assigned to macrostates. R²D locates thermodynamic direction in the boundary-defined possibility and constraint relation [2]. A complete reversed microstate may still realize an entropy-decreasing trajectory; the diagnostic separates that possibility from the enclosing statistical relation.

**Maxwell's demon.** Reading and using molecular distinctions requires a physical measurement and control architecture. The count domain must account for that architecture. Boundary accounting identifies an omitted resource, but a quantitative cost requires a specified physical cycle; distinguishability alone does not derive an erasure-work bound.

**Quantum measurement.** Resolving a previously subordinate distinction can change its classification from microstate to readable macrostate. R²D asks whether state identity is being assumed invariant through that operation. This supplies a structural diagnostic; quantitative quantum predictions require a physical mapping.

**Black-hole information.** A horizon changes external readability. R²D asks how internal state distinctions map to externally readable states. Loss of direct access alone does not settle unitarity or the correlations carried by Hawking radiation. A full resolution requires that mapping.

These extensions share a method, rather than an assertion that their complete physical solutions are already established.

## 10. A Taxonomy of the Assumed Invariant

| Problem | Relation whose preservation must be tested |
|---|---|
| Gibbs | Multiplicity after the counted distinction changes |
| Zeno, under a physical read | Readable coordinate through unlimited subdivision |
| Coastline | Scale-independent length and boundary-independent ruler |
| Mechanical $1/d$ divergence | Fixed entropy difference and accessible reversible landscape |
| Ultraviolet catastrophe | Classical thermal read across a change in relative recurrence and joint readability |
| Loschmidt | Identification of subordinate reversal with enclosing statistical direction |
| Maxwell's demon | A count domain that omits measurement and control resources |
| Quantum measurement | State classification through a change in readability |
| Black-hole information | The mapping between internal and external state distinctions |

The diagnostic proceeds in six steps:

1. Specify the count boundary $B_n$.
2. Define its readable macrostates and compatible subordinate realizations.
3. Identify the classification map and multiplicities.
4. Identify the physical read being extrapolated.
5. Specify the change in resolution, containment, capacity, or readability.
6. Test whether the conditions defining the proposed invariant survive that change.

If they fail, the original relation requires a new domain or a new physical read. If they survive, boundary projection has not explained the paradox, and further analysis is needed.

## 11. What the Example Establishes

Three levels of the argument should remain explicit.

**Within the R²D formalism**, statehood and multiplicity are boundary-relative. A change in boundary does not automatically preserve the classification or coordinate.

**Within the binary mechanical model**, the discrete force comparison establishes a finite threshold for the specified unmixing step. Its physical parameters enter through the mechanical system; they are not derived here from primitive combinatorics. The cited analysis is a theoretical treatment motivated by muscle, not an experimental measurement of an infinite-force limit.

**As a broader interpretation**, Gibbs, Zeno, and coastline expose related assumptions about continuing counts, coordinates, and rulers. The muscle model supplies one explicit capacity condition illustrating why such continuation can fail. Quantitative applications to other singularities remain open.

## 12. Conclusion

Gibbs, Zeno, and the coastline problem examine different mathematical reads of physical distinction. A count can fail when alternatives cease to be distinguishable. A coordinate can cease to resolve the same object when subdivision requires another boundary. A perimeter can change when the ruler and subordinate count change together.

Muscle provides a concrete mechanical bridge. The entropic-force coordinate contains a $1/d$ dependence, but the capacity to drive a specified unmixing step decreases as $\kappa_{\mathrm{sys}}d^2$. Their comparison establishes a finite limit on the reversible process before its formal continuation reaches $d=0$.

The common R²D principle is

$$
\boxed{
\text{Preservation of a physical read requires preservation of its defining state relation.}
}
$$

The coastline makes the geometric consequence explicit: the boundary defines its subordinate ruler. Gibbs makes the statistical consequence explicit: distinguishability defines what contributes to multiplicity. The mechanical example makes the operational consequence explicit: a finite landscape has a finite capacity to retain and reverse an entropic difference.

An apparent infinity can therefore indicate that a coordinate has been extended beyond the domain that gave it physical meaning. Establishing that diagnosis requires identifying the boundary and the failed distinction, not merely observing the divergence.
