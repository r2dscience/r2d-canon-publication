Density Boundary Coupling, Physical Volume, and the Source Read of Cosmological Peer Organization

Addendum 61 — Peer Energy and Boundary Support Occur Before Cosmological Energy

Abstract

Addendum 60 established that the Friedmann equation partitions one already-defined physical rate magnitude,

\[ H^2\]

into several downstream gravitational coordinates.

The matter-energy contribution is

\[ F_\epsilon=\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}\epsilon_\zeta\]

This raises the next projection problem.

What is

\[\epsilon_\zeta\]

in the R²D hierarchy?

It cannot be primitive.

Energy density already presupposes both a physical energy coordinate and a physical volume coordinate:

\[\epsilon_\zeta=\frac{E_\zeta}{V_\zeta}\]

R²D therefore requires two prior physical projections.

First, the compatible cosmological peer organization must acquire an energetic source read.

Second, its enclosing cosmological boundary must acquire a physical support-volume read.

Only their quotient is energy density.

Let

\(\pi_\zeta:\Omega_\zeta\to\mathcal M_\zeta\) be the cosmological macrostate classification.

A physical source-energy coordinate can represent cosmological macrostate i only if it factors through this classification.

Let

\[ E:\Gamma_G\to\mathbb R\]

be the physical energy read of a specified cosmological support domain.

Require

\[ E\circ\Pi_G=\bar E_\zeta\circ\pi_\zeta\]

Then macrostate i possesses the well-defined physical energy read

\[ E_{\zeta,i}=\bar E_\zeta(i)\]

This energy is not multiplicity.

It is not occupancy.

It is not peer number.

It is a later physical read of the recursively organized peer state.

The simplest source limit follows from the previously established peer-mass bridge.

For peer boundary

\[ B_{\zeta-1,\alpha}\]

let

\[\mu_\alpha\]

be its dimensionless orientation-even coupling and

\[ M_{\mathrm{cal}}\]

the physical mass calibration.

Then

\[ m_\alpha=M_{\mathrm{cal}}\mu_\alpha.\]

For a pressureless rest-mass-dominated peer ensemble whose source bookkeeping is additive,

\[ M_\zeta=\sum_\alpha m_\alpha=M_{\mathrm{cal}}\sum_\alpha\mu_\alpha.\]

and therefore

\[ E_\zeta^{\mathrm{dust}}=M_\zeta c^2=M_{\mathrm{cal}}c^2\sum_\alpha\mu_\alpha.\]

This is a physical limiting case.

The general relativistic source read is richer.

It can contain kinetic energy, momentum, pressure, stress, energy flux, and momentum flux in addition to rest energy.

Thus the mature relativistic source coordinate is not merely a sum of rest masses.

It is represented by the stress-energy structure of the projected peer organization.

The denominator is equally downstream.

Addendum 56 supplied the cosmological boundary-area read

\[ A_{\zeta,i}\]

For a fixed comoving FLRW support domain,

\[ A_\zeta=4\pi a^2S_k^2(\chi).\]

while the physical volume enclosed by that same comoving boundary is

\[ V_\zeta=4\pi a^3\int_0^\chi S_k^2(\chi')\,d\chi'\]

Therefore the support volume scales as

\[ V_\zeta\propto a^3.\]

whereas

\[ A_\zeta\propto a^2.\]

Eliminating a gives

\[ V_\zeta=\eta_{k,\chi}A_\zeta^{3/2}\]

where

\[\eta_{k,\chi}=\frac{1}{2\sqrt\pi}\frac{\int_0^\chi S_k^2(\chi')\,d\chi'}{S_k^3(\chi)}\]

is fixed by the chosen FLRW curvature class and comoving support domain.

In the spatially flat spherical limit,

\[ S_0(\chi)=\chi\]

so

\[\eta_{0,\chi}=\frac{1}{6\sqrt\pi}\]

recovering

\[ V=\frac43\pi r^3\]

from

\[ A=4\pi r^2\]

Thus the geometric hierarchy is

\[ B_\zeta\to A_\zeta\to V_\zeta\]

Physical volume does not define the primitive cosmological boundary.

It is the support coordinate derived from its geometric projection.

Only after both source energy and support volume have been defined does cosmological energy density appear:

\[\epsilon_{\zeta,i}=\frac{E_{\zeta,i}}{V_{\zeta,i}}\]

Using the area representation,

\[\epsilon_{\zeta,i}=\frac{E_{\zeta,i}}{\eta_{k,\chi}A_{\zeta,i}^{3/2}}\]

Energy density is therefore not a primitive amount of substance contained in pre-existing space.

It is a ratio between two physical reads of an already-defined cosmological state:

source-energy read

divided by

boundary-support read.

This relation also deprojects the matter-energy contribution to the Friedmann equation.

Define the gravitational length place value of the source energy,

\[\ell_{E,\zeta}\equiv\frac{GE_\zeta}{c^4}\]

Then

\[ F_\epsilon=\frac{8\pi G}{3c^2}\frac{E_\zeta}{V_\zeta}\]

becomes

\[ F_\epsilon=\frac{8\pi c^2\ell_{E,\zeta}}{3V_\zeta}\]

Or, using boundary area,

\[ F_\epsilon=\frac{8\pi c^2\ell_{E,\zeta}}{3\eta_{k,\chi}A_\zeta^{3/2}}\]

The conventional Friedmann source term can therefore be read as

gravitational place value of peer energy ÷ physical support of the enclosing boundary.

In the flat spherical limit,

\[ V=\frac43\pi r^3\]

so

\[ F_\epsilon=\frac{2c^2\ell_E}{r^3}\]

If

\[ E=Mc^2\]

then

\[ F_\epsilon=\frac{2GM}{r^3}\]

Thus the standard matter contribution is recovered from source-energy place value divided by boundary support.

The familiar cosmological dilution laws also become downstream consequences.

For a fixed comoving pressureless peer population with constant physical rest-energy read,

\[ E_\zeta^{\mathrm{dust}}=\text{constant}\]

Since

\[ V_\zeta\propto a^3.\]

we obtain

\[\epsilon_\zeta^{\mathrm{dust}}\propto a^{-3}\]

Or, because

\[ A_\zeta\propto a^2.\],

\[\epsilon_\zeta^{\mathrm{dust}}\propto A_\zeta^{-3/2}\]

The density decreases not because primitive peer count disappears but because a conserved physical source read occupies a larger projected support volume.

For a general homogeneous adiabatic source, conventional physical work gives

\[ dE_\zeta=-p_\zeta\,dV_\zeta.\]

where lower-case

\[ p_\zeta\]

is physical pressure.

It must not be confused with R²D possibility

\[ P_\zeta\]

Using

\[ E_\zeta=\epsilon_\zeta V_\zeta\]

one obtains

\[ V_\zeta\,d\epsilon_\zeta+(\epsilon_\zeta+p_\zeta)\,dV_\zeta=0\]

or

\[ d\epsilon_\zeta+(\epsilon_\zeta+p_\zeta)\,d\ln V_\zeta=0\]

Because

\[ V_\zeta\propto A_\zeta^{3/2}\]

\[ d\ln V_\zeta=\frac32d\ln A_\zeta\]

Therefore,

\[ d\epsilon_\zeta+\frac32(\epsilon_\zeta+p_\zeta)\,d\ln A_\zeta=0\]

Once physical clock calibration supplies

\[ H=\frac12\frac{d\ln A_\zeta}{dt}\]

the conventional continuity equation follows:

\[\dot\epsilon_\zeta+3H(\epsilon_\zeta+p_\zeta)=0\]

Thus cosmological source continuity can be read as the physical compatibility between changing peer- energy content and changing boundary support.

It does not establish primitive bottom-up causality.

The central statement is therefore:

cosmological energy density is not a primitive amount of substance distributed through pre-existing

And hence:

 peer energy and boundary support occur before cosmological energy density.

Addendum 60 isolated

The quantity

\[\epsilon_\zeta\]

is conventionally called energy density.

But density is already a ratio.

Therefore it cannot occupy the first causal level of an R²D derivation.

2. Energy Density Requires Energy

By definition,

\[\epsilon_\zeta=\frac{E_\zeta}{V_\zeta}\]

Thus a source-energy coordinate must exist before its density can be formed.

3. Energy Density Also Requires Physical Support

The denominator

\[ V\zeta\]

is a physical volume.

Therefore density also presupposes a geometric support coordinate.

The numerator and denominator have different projection origins.

\[\pi_\zeta:\Omega_\zeta\to\mathcal M_\zeta.\]

\[ E,\quad V,\quad\epsilon\]

For energy to represent cosmological macrostate i, require

\[ E\circ\Pi_G=\bar E_\zeta\circ\pi_\zeta\]

Then any two formal microstates belonging to the same macrostate must possess the same macrostate- level energy read.

7. Define the Macrostate Energy

When the factorization condition holds,

\[ E_{\zeta,i}=\bar E_\zeta(i)\]

This is the physical source-energy coordinate of cosmological state i.

8. Energy Is Not Peer Number

Let

\[ N_\zeta\]

\[ N_\zeta\ne E_{\zeta,i}\]

A peer count does not possess units of energy.

9. Energy Is Not Multiplicity

Likewise,

\[ W_{\zeta,i}\]

counts compatible joint peer realizations.

Therefore:

\[ W_{\zeta,i}\ne E_{\zeta,i}\]

Possible count and physical source energy are different objects.

The realized occupancy

\[ c_{\zeta,i}\]

Thus:

\[ c_{\zeta,i}\ne E_{\zeta,i}\]

No primitive count should be silently relabeled as energy.

The equation applies after mass and energy have been physically assigned to the peers.

The primitive peer architecture remains prior.
