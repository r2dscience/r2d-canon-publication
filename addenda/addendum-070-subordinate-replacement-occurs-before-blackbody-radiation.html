Addendum 70 — Subordinate Replacement Occurs Before Blackbody Radiation

Complementary Readability, Areal Recurrence Counting, and the Planck Spectrum

Abstract

Blackbody radiation is usually derived by counting electromagnetic modes inside a cavity.

R2D reverses the physical ordering.

The blackbody is first a thermodynamic object defined at a count boundary.

At that boundary:

  • temperature is readable;
  • occupancy is readable;
  • the radiative recurrence is readable through physical frequency;
  • but the continuous subordinate geometry realizing that recurrence is no longer jointly readable.

Addendum 3 identifies this as the quantum-side readability condition:

\[(\lambda,\nu)_Q=(\text{unreadable},\text{readable})\]

Blackbody radiation therefore occupies the transition from thermodynamic joint readability,

\[(\lambda,\nu)_T=(\text{readable},\text{readable})\]

toward recurrence-dominant quantum readability.

The causal ordering is not

\[\text{wave}\to\text{frequency}\to\text{photon}\to\text{blackbody spectrum}\]

R2D instead requires

subordinate closure→recursive replacement→readable radiative recurrence→frequency/phase representation→energetic and particle reads.

The blackbody boundary must therefore answer two distinct counting questions.

First:

How many times may each admissible radiative recurrence class be occupied?

R2D repeatable occupancy gives

\[ \bar m_f=\frac1{e^{hf/(k_BT)}-1}. \]

Second:

How many distinguishable radiative recurrence classes are admitted by the boundary at / frequency f ?

This second question supplies the geometric prefactor of the Planck spectrum.

Once closure recurrence has received a physical clock read f , Addendum 3 places the full-cycle energetic calibration later:

\[ E_f=hf. \]

Once that same recurrence has received the physical electromagnetic propagation calibration,

\[ \ell_f=\frac c f, \]

it acquires a downstream spatial recurrence ruler.

Define the corresponding recurrence area

\[ A_f=\ell_f^2=\frac{c^2}{f^2}. \]

Neither \(\ell_f\) nor Af is primitive R2D geometry.

They are physical coordinates assigned to a recurrence that already exists.

The blackbody boundary count can then be represented through transverse recurrence-phase distinguishability.

For one electromagnetic orientation class, a projected boundary element dA⊥ admits

\[ dN_\perp^{(1)}=\frac{f^2}{c^2}\,dA_\perp\,d\Omega \]

distinguishable directional recurrence classes.

Equivalently,

\[ dN_\perp^{(1)}=\frac{dA_\perp}{A_f}\,d\Omega\]

The factor

\[\frac{f^2}{c^2}\]

is therefore an areal recurrence-class count.

It does not require a primitive cavity volume.

A faster recurrence has a smaller physical recurrence-area ruler, so more mutually distinguishable recurrence classes can be resolved across the same projected boundary area.

Frequency distinguishability adds a second count.

During a clock-calibrated interval dt, recurrence frequencies separated by

\[ \Delta f\sim\frac1{dt} \]

are distinguishable.

Therefore:

\[ dN_f=dt\,df. \]

Combining directional, frequency, and electromagnetic orientation counts gives

\[ dN_{\mathrm{BB}}=g_{\mathrm{EM}}\frac{f^2}{c^2}\,dA_\perp\,d\Omega\,dt\,df. \]

Here

\[ g_{\mathrm{EM}}\]

is the number of independent electromagnetic orientation classes.

For observed electromagnetic radiation,

\[ g_{\mathrm{EM}}=2\]

R2D does not yet derive this factor from primitive retained-orientation counting.

It remains an empirical physical multiplicity.

Each admissible recurrence class has mean occupancy

\[\bar m_f=\frac1{e^{hf/(k_BT)}-1}\]

and each realized recurrence count has energetic read

\[ hf\]

Thus the energetic read crossing the boundary is

\[ dE=hf\,\bar m_f\,dN_{\mathrm{BB}}\]

Divide by projected boundary area, solid angle, clock interval, and frequency interval:

\[ B_f(T)=g_{\mathrm{EM}}\frac{hf^3}{c^2}\frac1{e^{hf/(k_BT)}-1}. \]

For

\[ g_{\mathrm{EM}}=2\]

\[ B_f(T)=\frac{2hf^3}{c^2}\frac1{e^{hf/(k_BT)}-1}\]

This is Planck’s spectral radiance law.

The result does not require a primitive cavity populated by independently existing electromagnetic modes.

The conventional cavity description remains valid as a later physical representation.

For isotropic radiation,

\[ u_f=\frac{4\pi}{c}B_f\]

so

\[ u_f(T)=\frac{8\pi hf^3}{c^3}\frac1{e^{hf/(k_BT)}-1}\]

The conventional cavity-state factor

\[ g(f)=\frac{8\pi f^2}{c^3}\]

is therefore recovered only after converting the boundary radiance read into a volume-density read.

R2D changes the explanatory order:

Blackbody boundary → recurrent-class count → repeatable occupancy → energetic read → radiance → volume density.

The cavity is not denied.

It is demoted from primitive ontology to a successful downstream representation of the same count.

The f 2 factor acquires a particularly direct R2D meaning.

Since

\[ A_f=\ell_f^2=\frac{c^2}{f^2}\]

\[\frac{f^2}{c^2}=\frac1{A_f}\]

Thus the spectral prefactor contains an areal recurrence count before it contains a volume mode density.

This does not mean the emitting surface is physically tiled by squares of area

\[ A_f\]

The recurrence area is a conversion ruler, not a primitive constituent.

The ratio

\[\frac{dA_\perp}{A_f}\]

counts distinguishable recurrent classes that the physical boundary can resolve.

The blackbody therefore provides a direct quantum-side application of Addendum 3.

Subordinate geometry has crossed the readability horizon.

Recurrence remains.

That recurrence is first counted, then represented through phase and frequency, then calibrated spatially through c, energetically through h, and finally read as radiative occupancy.

The central statement is therefore:

Archival box reconstructed for review. Blackbody radiation does not begin with photons or cavity modes. The blackbody is first a thermodynamic count boundary at which repeatable occupancy remains readable while subordinate radiative geometry has crossed the quantum readability horizon. The surviving recurrence acquires a clock-frequency read \(f\), a cycle-energy calibration \(E_f=hf\), and a spatial recurrence ruler \(\ell_f=c/f\). The blackbody boundary counts distinguishable recurrent classes areally, producing the \(f^2/c^2\) prefactor. Together with repeatable occupancy and energetic calibration, this yields Planck spectral radiance. Wave, photon, cavity mode, and volume density are later physical representations of the boundary count.

And hence:

subordinate replacement occurs before blackbody radiation.
